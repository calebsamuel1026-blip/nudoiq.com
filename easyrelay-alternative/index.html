What is Easy Relay?

EasyRelay is a Chrome extension for Amazon Relay dispatchers and carrier owners, published by Easy Relay LLC. Its store listing names load board tools such as an auto refresher, Newest to Top, Fast Book, tolls, load weight and Post a Truck. For trips, it lists current location, early or late ETAs and Telegram alerts. Its website adds an A+ Board and an Early Acceptance Check.

Does Easy Relay auto book loads?

Its store listing doesn't say it auto books your loads. As of October 9, 2026, automatic booking isn't on EasyRelay's listing or in any of its plans. Its website's description does mention auto-booking. The listing names Fast Book, for quick booking with a double-click. NudoIQ's AutoBook books for you, and only loads that clear your minimum net profit.

What does NudoIQ do that Easy Relay doesn't list?

Three things EasyRelay's listing and plans don't name: automatic booking at your net profit, net profit per load, and scorecard dispute letters.

  • AutoBook: set your minimum net profit (and $/mile, if you like). It books matching loads, skips any it can't fully price, and stops at your max-books cap.
  • Net profit on every load: net dollars and $/mile next to the price. It uses the costs you enter, like fuel, MPG, driver pay and maintenance.
  • Scorecard dispute letters: it drafts the letter from the violation. You read it, send it, and Amazon makes the call.
  • Also: Spanish, alerts or AutoBook on short-term contract offers, and Lane Intelligence, a 90-day price range for the lane.

What does Easy Relay do that NudoIQ doesn't?

As of October 9, 2026, EasyRelay costs less, has a free plan, and lists trip and board tools NudoIQ doesn't have.

  • What you pay: a $0 Free plan, or Pro Solo at $14.99 a month and Pro Team at $44.99. A 6-month option, it says, saves up to 42%.
  • Trip alerts on Telegram: yard checks, new and completed trips, road restrictions, driver check-ins and check-outs, new notes and ready trailers.
  • Trip details: driver app usage and trailer ID, plus a one-click copy of trip details.
  • Load board tools: tolls, latest pickup time, a Google Maps deadhead check and state exclusions (Pro, per its FAQ). Plus Auto Expand, Screenshot and Keyboard Shortcut.
  • Board highlights: bobtail, team and long-trip loads stand out right on the board, where NudoIQ shows only Team or Solo, in Pro View. EasyRelay Pro also marks loads where it expects price negotiation.
  • A+ Board and early check-in: its website says the A+ Board shows higher-score loads ahead of time. Its Early Acceptance Check shows if early check-in is possible.

How much does Easy Relay cost vs NudoIQ?

On EasyRelay, you can use a $0 Free plan. Its refresher comes with Pro Solo at $14.99 a month or Pro Team at $44.99 (checked October 9, 2026). NudoIQ costs more. It starts with a free preview on install, then a 7-day free trial with no card needed. After that it's $49.99 a month flat for every truck. Cancel anytime.

Should you pick Easy Relay or NudoIQ?

Pick EasyRelay if

  • You want a $0 Free plan, or Pro plans with the refresher from $14.99 a month.
  • You book each load by hand and want board tools like tolls, latest pickup time and deadhead checks.
  • You want trip alerts on Telegram for yard checks, road restrictions and driver check-ins.
  • You want the A+ Board or the Early Acceptance Check.

Pick NudoIQ if

  • You want loads booked for you, and only the ones that clear your minimum net profit.
  • You want to see what each load nets you before you take it.
  • You fight scorecard hits and want the dispute letter drafted for you.
  • You book Relay's short-term contracts, or you work in Spanish.

Does Easy Relay track trips?

Yes, EasyRelay's trip tools show your truck's current location, early or late ETAs, app usage and trailer ID. How does NudoIQ decide which loads to book?

In AutoBook mode, NudoIQ books only loads that clear your minimum net profit. If you set a minimum $/mile, they must clear that too. It never books a load it can't fully price, such as one missing a pickup time, miles or pay. It stops at your max-books cap. ALERT mode tells you about a match without booking it. That matters, because cancelling a booked load counts against you.

Does NudoIQ work in Spanish?
