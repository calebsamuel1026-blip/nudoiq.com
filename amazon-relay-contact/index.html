Amazon Relay phone number: how do you contact Amazon Relay?

Looking for an Amazon Relay phone number? Amazon says there isn't one. Here's where Amazon does send carriers, drivers and applicants, matched to the problem you have.

NudoIQ · Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Short answer

Amazon's Relay FAQ says there's no official Amazon Relay phone number. For a load in progress, reach the 24/7 Relay Operations Center (ROC) through the Relay portal or app. Account problems go to the Support Center when you're logged in.

Does Amazon Relay have a phone number?

No. Amazon's Relay FAQ says it plainly: "Amazon Relay doesn't have an official contact phone number."

People still search for one, with queries like "amazon relay phone number usa 24 7". No such number is published on relay.amazon.com. Treat any number you find on another site as unverified, and never give it your login.

One wrinkle. Amazon's free freight search post says you can "call our operations center" about delays or unexpected changes to a load. It doesn't print a number. So the way in is still the Relay portal or the Relay app.

How do you reach the Relay Operations Center?

Through Relay itself. The FAQ says: "Amazon provides 24/7 operational support for our carriers through the Relay Operations Center (ROC)." It adds that the ROC "can be reached through the Relay portal or Relay mobile."

Report schedule changes as they happen. Amazon's hours of service post says drivers and carriers "can use our app to connect with our operations for support," and that telling operations about a change helps your performance score reflect "only the factors within your control."

Amazon doesn't publish a response time for the ROC. "24/7" is the only service promise on the page.

How do drivers get help on the road?

In the app. Amazon's mobile app post says drivers can "request support from Relay Assistant throughout their trip," an AI chat tool for problems out on the road. "If they have a breakdown or mechanical issue, they can report it in the app."

The chat isn't a dead end. Amazon's September 2026 update says: "When Relay Assistant can't resolve an issue, it connects you to a human operator with your conversation's full context."

Who do you contact about your Relay account?

The Support Center. For account security, the FAQ says to "reach out to us through the Support Center when logged into your Relay account."

Can't log in at all? Our Amazon Relay login guide walks through Amazon's reset steps and how to spot a fake login page. If your account is suspended rather than locked, our account suspended guide covers what to check.

Who do you contact about an application?

Use Contact Us. The FAQ says to contact Relay support "by clicking Contact Us on the top right of your application page."

Before you do, check your FMCSA record. Amazon's onboarding team reaches applicants using the contact information on file with FMCSA. If that doesn't match your application, its messages can miss you.

How do you report fraud or double brokering?

By email. The FAQ says to "email relay-network-reports@amazon.com with a detailed description of the suspicious activity, including the DOT #s involved."

If you've been a victim of fraud, Amazon's geospoofing post also says to "file a report with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) at reportfraud.ftc.gov and your local law enforcement agency."

Check the address bar too. Amazon's email scams post says legitimate Relay websites have "relay.amazon.com" at the beginning of the URL.

Who handles ownership changes and privacy requests?

  • Ownership changes. The email scams post says to "email relay-ownership-transfers@amazon.com at least 30 days ahead."
  • Privacy and data requests. The FAQ says to contact transportation-privacy@amazon.com.

Who do you contact about pay or a scorecard hit?

Pay problems go through the portal. The FAQ says you can submit payment disputes "directly through Amazon Relay portal within 30 days of reviewing your weekly invoice." Our Amazon Relay disputes guide lists each dispute type and where Amazon says to file it.

For a late arrival or app usage hit you think is wrong, our free Relay dispute letter tool lets you tick what's true and copy a letter to submit. Amazon decides.

Do Relay Rewards carriers get faster support?

Amazon's Relay Rewards post lists "Dedicated office hours" with "priority assistance from Amazon Relay's support team" among the program's benefits. It doesn't say which tier gets them or publish the hours. That post says the program is US only, though relay.amazon.ca also lists "Rewards and Discounts" without details.

In Canada, relay.amazon.ca gives the same ROC answer: it's reached through the Relay portal or Relay mobile.

What doesn't Amazon publish about contacting Relay?

  • Any phone number, for the US, Canada or "24/7" support
  • ROC hours beyond "24/7," or any response time
  • A route for someone fully locked out, beyond the account recovery steps
  • A mailing address for carriers
  • A dispute phone number

If a site gives you a number for any of these, it didn't come from Amazon's Relay pages.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an Amazon Relay customer service phone number?

No, Amazon Relay has no customer service line. Amazon's Relay FAQ says Amazon Relay doesn't have an official contact phone number. Load problems go to the 24/7 Relay Operations Center through the Relay portal or Relay mobile, and account problems go to the Support Center when you're logged in.

What is the Amazon Relay ROC phone number?

Amazon doesn't publish a number for the Relay Operations Center. Its FAQ says the Relay Operations Center (ROC) gives 24/7 support and is reached through the Relay portal or Relay mobile. Treat any ROC number on a third-party site as unverified.

What is the Amazon Relay support email?

Amazon Relay publishes no general support email. The emails it does publish are for specific jobs: relay-network-reports@amazon.com for suspicious activity, relay-ownership-transfers@amazon.com for ownership changes and transportation-privacy@amazon.com for privacy requests. Account help goes through the Support Center while logged in.

Is there an Amazon Relay phone number for drivers?

No, drivers get help in the Relay app. They can chat with Relay Assistant during a trip and report breakdowns there, and Amazon says Relay Assistant connects them to a human operator when it can't resolve an issue.

What is the Amazon Relay contact number in Canada?

Amazon doesn't publish a Relay phone number for Canada. The relay.amazon.ca page says the 24/7 Relay Operations Center is reached through the Relay portal or Relay mobile, the same as in the US.

How do I contact Amazon Relay about my application?

Click Contact Us on the top right of your application page, as Amazon's FAQ says. If you haven't heard back in two weeks, first check that your FMCSA contact information matches your application exactly, since Amazon's onboarding team uses it to reach you.
