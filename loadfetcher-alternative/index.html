What is Load Fetcher?

LoadFetcher is a Chrome extension for Amazon Relay carriers, owner-operators and dispatchers, built into the Relay load board. Fierce Trade LLC publishes it. Its store listing, checked October 9, 2026, names an auto refresher, alerts, load pricing and history, and trip tracking. It also names a Post Truck Manager, maps and routes, and road restriction and facility info.

Does Load Fetcher auto book loads?

Its public listing doesn't say it does. As of October 9, 2026, automatic booking isn't on LoadFetcher's store listing or plans section. Its website's title does mention auto booking. NudoIQ's AutoBook books for you, and only loads that clear your Min Net Profit.

What does NudoIQ do that Load Fetcher doesn't list?

Three things: automatic booking at your own profit floor, net profit on every load card, and scorecard dispute letters.

  • AutoBook: set your minimum net profit and your max-books cap. It books matching loads and skips any load it can't fully price.
  • Net profit on every load: net dollars and $/mile right next to the price, using the costs you enter.
  • Scorecard dispute letters: it drafts the letter from the violation. You read it, send it, and Amazon makes the call.
  • Also: English and Spanish, Instant Book on new and price-up loads, Lane Intelligence (90-day lane price range), backhaul search, One-Click Post A Truck.

What does Load Fetcher do that NudoIQ doesn't?

As of October 9, 2026, LoadFetcher lists a Free plan at $0 a month, a shared team workspace and yearly billing. Its listing also names alerts for road restrictions, late ETAs and driver check-ins. NudoIQ has none of those.

How much does Load Fetcher cost vs NudoIQ?

LoadFetcher: Free, Plus $24, Pro $49, Team $90 a month ($45 a seat), with a yearly option, checked October 9, 2026. NudoIQ: a free preview on install, then a 7-day free trial with no card needed to start. After that it's $49.99 a month flat for every truck. Cancel anytime.

Should you pick Load Fetcher or NudoIQ?

Pick LoadFetcher if

  • You want to stay on a free plan. Its Free plan has a refresher with filters and Post a Truck tools.
  • You book every load by hand and just need to see them first.
  • You want trip alerts for road restrictions, late ETAs and driver check-ins.
  • You run a team that needs a shared workspace and seat management.

Pick NudoIQ if

  • You want loads booked for you, and only the ones that clear your minimum net profit.
  • You want to see what each load nets you before you take it.
  • You fight scorecard hits and want the dispute letter drafted for you.
  • You run several trucks and want one flat price, or you work in Spanish.
