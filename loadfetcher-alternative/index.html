LoadFetcher vs NudoIQ: What's the Difference

Both LoadFetcher and NudoIQ refresh the Amazon Relay load board and book loads automatically. But they offer different feature sets and price points. Here's how they compare.

What Both Tools Do

LoadFetcher and NudoIQ are both Amazon Relay extensions. They both live in your browser, run on desktop Chrome, and do the same core job: refresh the load board automatically so you don't have to, and book matching loads for you if you set it up.

That's the table-stakes feature set for any Relay extension. The load board refreshes when you reload it, good loads book within seconds, and manual refreshing every 30 seconds doesn't work if you're actually driving or managing your business. A refresher extension solves that.

Both tools let you set booking rules—pick loads by lane, price, distance, equipment—and book automatically when a match posts. Both track your profit floor (the minimum payout you'll accept) and a maximum-books cap (stop booking once you hit it).

Where LoadFetcher Stops

LoadFetcher's pitch is simplicity. It offers a free tier that includes the refresher and basic booking rules. If you only need to reload the board automatically and book loads that match your filters, LoadFetcher's free version does that.

LoadFetcher does not advertise lane rate history (what that route normally pays), net profit per load (what you keep after costs), scorecard dispute letters, backhaul finding, or Post A Truck helpers. Those features are not part of LoadFetcher's public offering.

For a carrier who wants a refresher and basic booker, LoadFetcher's free tier is a zero-dollar way to work smarter than manual refreshing.

Where NudoIQ Adds

NudoIQ starts where LoadFetcher's free tier ends. Every feature is included at one price: $49.99 a month.

Lane Intelligence: NudoIQ checks the posted rate against that lane's 90-day price history. It shows you a target rate (what the lane normally pays), an expected range, and a confidence level. You see, for example, "$483 target next to a $407 board price." This is decision support, not a guarantee—Amazon sets rates based on season, time of day, volume, and other factors—but it tells you whether today's rate is high or low for that specific route.

Net Profit Per Load: Instead of just seeing the posted rate, you see the dollars you keep after subtracting driver pay, fuel, maintenance, and deadhead to pickup. The extension calculates this for every load card, so you can set a profit floor and hide loads that don't clear it. A $600 rate might net $200 after costs on one lane and $400 on another. NudoIQ shows you the actual take-home.

Scorecard Dispute Letters: Your performance scorecard in Relay tracks safety, on-time delivery, tender acceptance, and app usage. Low scores limit your access to loads. If Amazon dings your scorecard and you disagree with the violation, NudoIQ reads your scorecard, identifies the violation, and drafts a dispute letter citing Amazon policy. You review it, edit it if needed, and submit it yourself. Amazon makes the final decision, but NudoIQ saves you hours of writing and research.

Backhaul Finder: Once you complete a load and you're at the drop-off, NudoIQ searches for loads from that location back toward home (within your radius and time window). It sorts by total pay, rate per mile, pickup time, or distance home, so you can find a profitable return trip quickly instead of scrolling the entire board.

Post A Truck Helper: When you post availability on Relay's Post A Truck feature, NudoIQ shows your origin, destination, distance, and board price next to your minimum $/mile and minimum payout. Then it posts with one button.

Other Conveniences: HOS overview, driver dispatch by text/email on assignment, control bar with three modes (Alert to ping you on a match, Search to highlight matches, Autobook to book automatically).

Who Should Stay on LoadFetcher's Free Tier

If you only need a refresher and basic booking rules, LoadFetcher's free tier does the job. You don't need lane rate history, net profit calculations, or dispute automation if:

  • You already know what lanes pay from experience.
  • You calculate profit in your head or a spreadsheet.
  • Your scorecard is clean and you don't expect to dispute anything.
  • You rarely book backhauls and find return trips manually.
  • You don't use Post A Truck.

For those carriers, LoadFetcher's free tool is sufficient and costs zero dollars. There's no reason to pay for features you don't use.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use both LoadFetcher and NudoIQ at the same time?

Both extensions run inside the same load board tab, so installing both could cause conflicts or unexpected behavior. Use one or the other, not both. You can always switch if you want to test different tools.

Does NudoIQ slow down the load board?

No. NudoIQ runs inside the Relay tab and doesn't throttle the board's performance. It adds a control bar and calculates profit and lane data in the background, but it doesn't affect how fast the board loads or refreshes.

What if I use LoadFetcher now and want to switch to NudoIQ?

Remove LoadFetcher from your extensions, then install NudoIQ from the Chrome Web Store. Sign up for the 7-day free trial, and you'll see all the features. Your Relay login and load board stay the same—you're just changing the extension.

Does NudoIQ tell me which loads to book?

No. It shows you rate history, profit, and dispute data, but the booking decision is yours. NudoIQ is decision support. You set the rules (price, lane, minimum profit), and it alerts you or books automatically based on those rules. Amazon Relay is your business; the tool helps you run it smarter.

Is there a discount if I sign up for longer than one month?

NudoIQ's pricing is month-to-month at $49.99. There's no annual pricing or discount. You pay for the month and can cancel anytime.

How do I know if lane rate history is accurate?

Lane rate history in NudoIQ comes from the Relay load board's posted rates over the past 90 days. It's not a prediction—it's what that lane actually paid. But Amazon's rates change based on season, volume, time of day, and other factors, so historical rates don't guarantee today's rate. That's why NudoIQ calls it decision support, not a guarantee.
