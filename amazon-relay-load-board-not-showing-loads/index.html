When the Amazon Relay Load Board Shows No Loads

An empty board means no work available right now. Check your filters and account status in order before contacting support.

Check Filters First

The load board uses filters to show work that matches your truck and needs. If you see nothing, a filter is likely set too narrow.

Open your filters and verify:

  • Equipment type: Matches what you run. A reefer filter shows only refrigerated loads. A dry van filter shows only dry work.
  • Load type: You have not excluded the work you want.
  • Location radius: You set it wide enough to see loads near you.
  • Trip length: Set to the distance you want to haul.
  • Pickup and delivery windows: Include times you can work.
  • Rate range: Not filtering out available loads.
  • Trailer status, driver type, layout, maximum stops: All set for your operation.

The board allows you to save searches so you do not enter filters every time. Check if an old saved search is active and showing fewer results.

Reset filters to see the full board. Then add them back to narrow the view.

Check Your Account Status

If your insurance coverage lapses, you cannot book or haul loads until it is reinstated. This blocks the board.

Amazon continually monitors carrier compliance using internal teams and a third-party service. If your account has a compliance flag, the board may be restricted.

Check the Relay portal:

  • Log out and log back in to refresh your session.
  • Look for any warnings or notices about insurance, authority status, or compliance.
  • Go to the Insurance tab. If your policy is expiring soon, renew it two to three weeks before expiry and update your account at least 5 days before the policy ends.

If you see a notice about compliance or insurance, follow the instructions in that notice. Contact support via the Support Center (in your Relay portal when logged in).

Browser Troubleshooting

Try the usual steps:

  • Close the browser completely.
  • Open a new browser window and log in to Relay again.
  • Disable all Chrome extensions one at a time to see if one is interfering with the board display.
  • Try a different browser (Firefox, Safari, Edge) to rule out a Chrome-specific issue.

When to Contact Support

Contact the Relay Operations Center (ROC) through your Relay portal if the board is still empty after you have checked filters and account status. Provide your Relay ID and a screenshot of what you see.

The Refresh Rate

The Relay load board auto-refreshes and shows thousands of new spot freight matches every 30 seconds. If you are watching the board in real time, new loads appear every half minute.

NudoIQ Auto-Refresh reloads the board every few seconds and the speed is adjustable, so you do not have to watch manually or refresh constantly.

Frequently asked questions

Does my performance scorecard affect what loads I see?

Yes. High-performing carriers get first access to loads. Your scorecard is in the Relay portal under Performance.

Can a saved search cause the board to look empty?

Yes. If an old saved search is active with narrow filters, it may not match current loads. Reset your saved search or create a new one with wider criteria.

What if my board shows a few loads but not many?

Your area may have fewer loads at that time. Try widening your radius or adjusting your pickup and delivery windows.

Who do I contact for questions about board access?

Contact the Relay Operations Center (ROC) through the Relay portal. For login or account-access issues, use the Support Center in the Relay portal.

Where do I see my performance rating?

Your scorecard is in the Relay portal under Performance.
