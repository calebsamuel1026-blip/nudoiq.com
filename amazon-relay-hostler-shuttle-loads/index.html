Amazon Relay shuttle and hostler loads: how they work

Hostling and shuttle runs are Relay's local trailer moves, booked by the block instead of the load. Here's how they work, what you need, and what Amazon leaves out.

NudoIQ · Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Short answer

Hostling on Amazon Relay means moving trailers within one Amazon location, and shuttle runs move trailers between nearby Amazon sites, usually within 50 miles. You book both by blocks of time, and Amazon says you're paid for the full block.

What are hostler and shuttle loads on Amazon Relay?

Local trailer moves. Amazon's hostler post puts it simply: if you've ever moved trailers around a yard, dock or warehouse site, that's hostling.

Relay has two kinds:

  • Single-site hostling: "Moving trailers within one Amazon location." For example, positioning a trailer for loading or unloading at a delivery station.
  • Shuttle runs: "Moving trailers between nearby Amazon sites (usually within 50 miles)." For example, hauling trailers from a busy fulfillment or sort center to an offsite storage lot.

Amazon says most carriers handle both trailer staging and swapping during a block.

They're new. Amazon's RelayCon 2025 write-up says hostling and shuttle work launched in 2025, and that "they're power-only." Our power only guide covers the wider power-only side of Relay.

How do hostling blocks work?

You book time, not loads. "You'll book these moves by blocks of time instead of individual loads." Your driver handles whatever trailer moves come up during the slot.

What Amazon says about blocks:

  • Pay. "You'll be paid for the full block of time that you commit to," even if Amazon doesn't use all the time.
  • More than one a day. "One driver can work multiple blocks per day as long as they don't overlap."
  • A hard cap. Drivers must stay within their hours of service limits, with a 12-hour maximum on hostling support. Amazon says keeping drivers inside those limits is the carrier's job.
  • Booking limits. "There's a cap on the amount of hostling moves you can book," so the work spreads across eligible carriers.

What do you need for Amazon Relay hostling and shuttle runs?

On top of being an approved Relay carrier, Amazon lists:

  1. Your own day cab or hostler truck
  2. A valid CDL for the driver, since "public roads may be used to access offsite yards"
  3. A C+ performance grade
  4. Two Learning Center courses: one on hostling services for Amazon and one on Relay auctions

You need to be approved before you can take the courses. Our scorecard guide explains what sets your grade.

How do you book hostling and shuttle moves?

Two ways, per Amazon.

On the load board. "Just filter by power-only loads and pick the times and locations that work for you (e.g., BNA5 -> BNA5)." In Amazon's example, the move starts and ends at the same site, which reads like single-site hostling. That's our reading. Amazon says you see start and end times up front.

Through auctions. You can bid on hostling and shuttle lanes. "Relay will calculate the hourly rate per driver as you place a bid." And "When your company wins an auction, you'll get up to 6 months of moves." Our auctions guide covers how Relay bidding works.

Weighing a block against a regular power-only load? Our free Relay load calculator shows a load's net and net per loaded mile from its pay and miles.

Who are hostler and shuttle loads a fit for?

Amazon pitches them to carriers with idle day cabs who want local work closer to home. It also says, in a September 2026 post, that newer options like "hostler and shuttle and third-party shipper loads" help carriers diversify.

The trade-off is control. You commit a driver and a truck to a block, and Amazon decides which moves fill it.

What doesn't Amazon publish about hostling?

  • Hourly rates
  • The booking cap number
  • Which facilities offer hostling
  • Specs for hostler trucks (yard tractors)
  • Whether a shuttle run needs a sleeper

Frequently asked questions

What is shuttle work on Amazon Relay?

Shuttle runs move trailers between nearby Amazon sites, usually within 50 miles, such as from a fulfillment center to an offsite storage lot. They're power-only and booked by blocks of time.

What is a shuttle request on Amazon Relay?

We found no public Amazon Relay page that uses the term. Shuttle moves are booked as blocks of time on the load board or won through auctions.

What do I need to do hostling on Amazon Relay?

Amazon lists your own day cab or hostler truck, a valid CDL for the driver, a C+ performance grade and two Learning Center courses, one on hostling and one on Relay auctions.

How long can a driver work hostling blocks on Relay?

One driver can work several blocks a day if they don't overlap, but Amazon says drivers must stay within their hours of service limits, with a 12-hour maximum on hostling support.

Are Amazon Relay shuttle loads paid by the hour?

Shuttle loads are booked and paid by blocks of time. Amazon says you're paid for the full block you commit to, and that Relay calculates an hourly rate per driver when you bid in an auction. It doesn't publish rates.

How do I find shuttle loads on the Amazon Relay load board?

Amazon says to filter by power-only loads and pick times and locations that work for you. Amazon's own example is BNA5 to BNA5, a move that starts and ends at the same site.
