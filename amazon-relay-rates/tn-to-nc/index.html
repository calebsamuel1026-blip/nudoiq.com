Frequently asked questions

How much do Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to North Carolina pay?

One-way Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to North Carolina had a median final board price of $407 and a median of $1.98 per mile. Half of the 70 one-way loads seen July 7 to October 5, 2026, fell between $1.80 and $2.50 per mile. The median one-way trip was 231 miles. Only 2 round trips were seen, too few to report a rate.

Does Amazon raise the price on TN to NC Relay loads that sit on the board?

Yes, on 66% of TN to NC loads the board price went up at least once while they were posted. More than half (58%) ended above their first price, with a median rise of 17.3%. Prices also fell, with 46% cut at least once and 20% ending below where they started.

Does the time of day change Amazon Relay rates from Tennessee to North Carolina?

With only 74 TN to NC loads seen on 28 days, there is not enough data to tell if time of day changes the rate. Weekday and weekend loads showed no clear difference.

Where do these Amazon Relay rate numbers come from?

They come from 74 TN to NC loads that NudoIQ users saw on the Amazon Relay board from July 7 to October 5, 2026. Each load counts once, at the last price seen, and all of them started in Louisville, TN (74 of 74), where NudoIQ users search. These are observed board prices, not earnings. Amazon did not provide this data, and NudoIQ is not affiliated with Amazon.
