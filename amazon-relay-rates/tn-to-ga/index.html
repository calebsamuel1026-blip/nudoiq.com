Frequently asked questions

How much do Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Georgia pay?

One-way Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Georgia had a median final board price of $400 and a median of $2.80 per mile. Half of the 316 one-way loads seen July 7 to October 5, 2026, fell between $2.00 and $3.43 per mile. The median one-way trip was 146 miles. Only 13 round trips were seen, too few to report a rate.

How long do TN to GA loads stay on the Amazon Relay board?

The median TN to GA load stayed on the board 1.6 hours after a NudoIQ user first saw it (125 loads seen leaving). Of those, 39% were gone within 30 minutes, but 42% were still posted after 4 hours. Leaving the board does not prove a load was booked, and some loads were posted before they were first seen.

Does Amazon raise the price on TN to GA Relay loads that sit on the board?

Yes, on 59% of TN to GA loads the board price went up at least once while they were posted. Just over half (54%) ended above their first price, with a median rise of 13.9%. Prices were also cut at least once on 26% of loads, and 7% ended below where they started.

Does the time of day change Amazon Relay rates from Tennessee to Georgia?

No clear difference showed up by time of day in 316 one-way TN to GA loads seen from July 7 to October 5, 2026. Weekdays and weekends showed no clear difference either. Times are based on when each load was first seen, which can be after Amazon posted it.

Where do these Amazon Relay rate numbers come from?

They come from 356 TN to GA loads that NudoIQ users saw on the Amazon Relay board from July 7 to October 5, 2026. Each load counts once, at the last price seen, and more than half started in Maryville, TN (190 of 356), where NudoIQ users search. These are observed board prices, not earnings. Amazon did not provide this data, and NudoIQ is not affiliated with Amazon.
