Amazon Relay rates: Tennessee to Kentucky (TN to KY)

On the Amazon Relay board, one-way TN to KY loads had a median of $2.81 per mile and $552 per load. The busiest run, Maryville to Columbia, KY, had a median of $2.55 per mile across 61 loads.

NudoIQ data · Loads first seen July 7, 2026 to October 5, 2026 · Published October 5, 2026 · Last updated October 5, 2026

Short answer

NudoIQ users saw 662 distinct Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Kentucky first seen from July 7 to October 5, 2026. One-way loads had a median final board price of $552 and a median of $2.81 per mile (median trip 202 miles; middle half of loads: $2.47 to $3.12 per mile). Round trips had a median of $619 and $2.32 per mile (median 285 miles, counting the drive back). 74% of loads were raised in price at least once while posted.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Kentucky pay?

One-way Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Kentucky had a median final board price of $552 and a median of $2.81 per mile. Half of the 487 one-way loads seen July 7 to October 5, 2026, fell between $2.47 and $3.12 per mile. The median one-way trip was 202 miles. The 35 round trips had a median of $619 and $2.32 per mile, counting the drive back.

How long do TN to KY loads stay on the Amazon Relay board?

The median TN to KY load stayed on the board 5.8 hours after a NudoIQ user first saw it (340 loads seen leaving). Of those, only 19% were gone within 30 minutes, and 60% were still posted after 4 hours. Leaving the board does not prove a load was booked, and some loads were posted before they were first seen.

Does Amazon raise the price on TN to KY Relay loads that sit on the board?

Yes, on 74% of TN to KY loads the board price went up at least once while they were posted. Most (72%) ended above their first price, with a median rise of 23.6%. Prices were also cut at least once on 51% of loads, but only 4% ended below where they started.

Do round trips pay less per mile than one-way loads from Tennessee to Kentucky?

Yes, TN to KY round trips had a median of $2.32 per mile, compared with $2.81 for one-way loads. Round-trip miles include the drive back, and in dollars the median round trip was higher, $619 against $552. This lane had only 35 round trips and 487 one-way loads. Across all lanes, round trips were priced at a median 0.68 times the one-way rate per mile on the same city pair.

Where do these Amazon Relay rate numbers come from?

They come from 662 TN to KY loads that NudoIQ users saw on the Amazon Relay board from July 7 to October 5, 2026. Each load counts once, at the last price seen, and most started in Maryville, TN (615 of 662), where NudoIQ users search. These are observed board prices, not earnings. Amazon did not provide this data, and NudoIQ is not affiliated with Amazon.
