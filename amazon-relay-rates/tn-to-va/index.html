Frequently asked questions

How much do Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Virginia pay?

One-way Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Virginia had a median final board price of $537 and a median of $2.94 per mile. Half of the 170 one-way loads seen July 25 to October 4, 2026, fell between $2.68 and $3.08 per mile. The median one-way trip was 189 miles. Only 4 round trips were seen, too few to report a rate.

How long do TN to VA loads stay on the Amazon Relay board?

The median TN to VA load stayed on the board 6.4 hours after a NudoIQ user first saw it (78 loads seen leaving). Of those, only 10% were gone within 30 minutes, and 63% were still posted after 4 hours. Leaving the board does not prove a load was booked, and some loads were posted before they were first seen.

Does Amazon raise the price on TN to VA Relay loads that sit on the board?

Yes, on 78% of TN to VA loads the board price went up at least once while they were posted. Most (77%) ended above their first price, with a median rise of 21.6%. Prices were also cut at least once on 48% of loads, but only 1% ended below where they started.

Does the time of day change Amazon Relay rates from Tennessee to Virginia?

No clear difference showed up by time of day in 170 one-way TN to VA loads seen from July 25 to October 4, 2026. Weekdays and weekends showed no clear difference either. Times are based on when each load was first seen, which can be after Amazon posted it.

Where do these Amazon Relay rate numbers come from?

They come from 181 TN to VA loads that NudoIQ users saw on the Amazon Relay board from July 25 to October 4, 2026. Each load counts once, at the last price seen, and all of them started in Maryville, TN (181 of 181), where NudoIQ users search. These are observed board prices, not earnings. Amazon did not provide this data, and NudoIQ is not affiliated with Amazon.
