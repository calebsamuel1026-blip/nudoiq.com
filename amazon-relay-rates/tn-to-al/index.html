Frequently asked questions

How much do Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Alabama pay?

One-way Amazon Relay loads from Tennessee to Alabama had a median final board price of $465 and a median of $3.01 per mile. Half of the 108 one-way loads seen July 25 to October 5, 2026, fell between $2.36 and $3.29 per mile. The median one-way trip was 161 miles. Only 8 round trips were seen, too few to report a rate.

How long do TN to AL loads stay on the Amazon Relay board?

The median TN to AL load stayed on the board 54 minutes after a NudoIQ user first saw it (40 loads seen leaving). Of those, almost half (48%) were gone within 30 minutes, while 28% were still posted after 4 hours. Leaving the board does not prove a load was booked, and some loads were posted before they were first seen.

Does Amazon raise the price on TN to AL Relay loads that sit on the board?

Yes, on 49% of TN to AL loads the board price went up at least once while they were posted. Just under half (46%) ended above their first price, with a median rise of 25.3%. Prices were also cut at least once on 28% of loads, and 6% ended below where they started.

Does the time of day change Amazon Relay rates from Tennessee to Alabama?

Yes, one-way TN to AL loads first seen at night had a median of $3.18 per mile, compared with $2.51 in the afternoon. Evening loads had a median of $3.16. At the same trip length, night ran 29.2% and evening 28.1% above the afternoon. Times are Eastern, and this lane has only 136 loads seen on 33 days, so treat the pattern as a guide.

Where do these Amazon Relay rate numbers come from?

They come from 136 TN to AL loads that NudoIQ users saw on the Amazon Relay board from July 25 to October 5, 2026. Each load counts once, at the last price seen, and most started in Maryville, TN (81 of 136), where NudoIQ users search. These are observed board prices, not earnings. Amazon did not provide this data, and NudoIQ is not affiliated with Amazon.
