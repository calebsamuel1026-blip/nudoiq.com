Amazon Relay peak season 2026: dates, load volume and how to prepare

Peak season is here for Relay carriers. Prime Big Deal Days ran October 6 and 7, and Amazon expects load volume to keep climbing into December. Here's what Amazon has said about the 2026 season and how to get ready.

NudoIQ · Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Short answer

Amazon says Relay load volume spikes between October and January, and that in 2026 it expects over 4 million loads, with 300,000 a week by December. Those are Amazon's freight volume numbers, not a forecast of carrier pay.

When is Amazon Relay peak season?

October through January. Amazon's peak season post says "load volume spikes during the 'haulidays' between October and January."

It names four events that drive the volume:

EventWhen
Prime Big Deal DaysOctober 6 and 7, 2026, per Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days post. Already done.
Black Friday and Cyber MondayAmazon runs major weeks of sales around the Friday and Monday after Thanksgiving. In 2026 those days are November 27 and November 30.
Super SaturdayThe last Saturday before Christmas. In 2026 that's December 19.
ChristmasDecember 25

The calendar dates for the last three are ours, worked out from Amazon's descriptions. Amazon's Relay posts don't give 2026 dates for those sales.

Prime Big Deal Days being over doesn't end anything. Amazon says "the loads will continue even after Prime Big Deal Days has ended."

How many loads does Amazon expect in 2026?

Amazon's peak season post says: "in 2026, we're expecting over 4 million loads," and "300 thousand per week by December." Amazon's brokers vs direct booking post describes Relay as "Thousands of loads during regular periods, nearly doubling during peak."

Amazon gives the 4 million figure for 2026 as a whole. It doesn't say how many of those loads fall in peak season. Read these for what they are. They're Amazon's numbers about freight volume. They say nothing about rates or what any carrier takes home, and Amazon publishes no peak rates.

What kind of freight? Amazon's winter loads post says the holidays are Amazon's busiest season, "where the majority of our loads are power-only."

How do you book peak freight on Amazon Relay?

Amazon names these options for peak:

  • Load board. "Relay's load board auto-refreshes every 30 seconds with a list of available spot freight."
  • Post A Truck. Build a criteria order with your distance, duration, payment and more, and Relay matches you to loads automatically.
  • Contracts. Short-term contracts give weeks to months of steady round trips in one booking.
  • Auctions. Bid on a contract auction for a chance to name your own price.
  • Equipment marketplace. Rent trailers to Amazon.

You can also turn on load alerts. Amazon's Prime Day post describes instant notifications for loads "that meet your business's criteria." And the Prime Big Deal Days post suggests peak may be a good time to look at team driving or intermodal.

Does your scorecard matter more during peak?

It decides how much you can book. Amazon's high volume events post says your performance score "determines how much and how often an approved carrier is able to book Amazon loads." A healthy score gives "access to the most freight opportunities during high volume events (including early access)."

Two things from that post help here:

  • Scores refresh every week. You also get "a daily, real-time prediction of your upcoming score" in your account.
  • Breakdowns can hit your score. If a breakdown makes you late or forces you to reject a booked load, "your performance score can be impacted."

Amazon doesn't say whether scorecard rules change during peak. Our scorecard guide covers how each metric works.

How should carriers prepare for peak?

Here's Amazon's own list, from the high volume events post, plus one step of ours:

  1. Update your contact info. Keep your primary administrator's details current, so booking alerts and problem notices reach you.
  2. Plan a backup. Amazon suggests extra drivers or trucks for sick days and breakdowns, and says Relay has discounts for truck rentals.
  3. Confirm load details before you book. "Only book loads your company can confidently execute." Amazon says you'll usually know the equipment, arrival time, rate, locations and driver type before you commit.
  4. Do preventive maintenance now. Follow service schedules, and run pre- and post-trip inspections. Drivers can report equipment issues in the Relay app.
  5. Plan around hours of service. Amazon says Relay navigation factors HOS into tour planning. Its hours of service post also says to account for seasonal delays, especially between November and February.
  6. Check each load's numbers. Our free Relay load calculator shows a load's net and net per loaded mile from its pay and miles, deadhead included.

Is it too late to join Amazon Relay for peak 2026?

Not necessarily, but approval takes time. Amazon's Relay FAQ says applications are typically approved or rejected within 1 to 3 weeks, and other Amazon posts say within three weeks or 2 to 3 weeks. So plan on up to three. Apply in mid-October and a decision could come by early November, ahead of Black Friday week.

Two more things take time. Each driver has to clear an identity check, which Amazon typically reviews within 48 hours, plus a background check. And if you're an existing carrier changing insurance, Amazon recommends allowing 2 weeks for it to process.

Amazon's five application steps are in our guide to how to sign up for Amazon Relay.

What doesn't Amazon publish about peak season?

  • Peak rates or rate changes
  • Cut-off dates for booking or applying
  • Whether scorecard rules change during peak
  • Load numbers for Super Saturday or Black Friday 2026
  • 2026 dates for Black Friday, Cyber Monday or Super Saturday sales, in its Relay posts

Frequently asked questions

When is Amazon Relay peak season?

Amazon says Relay load volume spikes between October and January. The events it names are Prime Big Deal Days, Black Friday and Cyber Monday, Super Saturday and Christmas.

How many Amazon Relay loads does Amazon expect in 2026?

Amazon's peak season post says it expects over 4 million loads in 2026, with 300 thousand per week by December. It doesn't say how many of those fall in peak. Those are freight volume numbers, not a forecast of carrier pay.

Does Amazon Relay pay more during peak season?

Amazon Relay doesn't publish peak rates or rate changes. Its peak posts give load volume forecasts only. Check each load's price against your own costs before you book.

When was Prime Big Deal Days 2026 on Amazon Relay?

For Relay carriers, Amazon says Prime Big Deal Days 2026 ran Tuesday, October 6, through Wednesday, October 7, 2026. It adds that loads continue after the event ends.

How long is Amazon Relay peak season?

Relay peak runs about four months. Amazon's peak season post says load volume spikes between October and January, and says the freight lasts long after the holidays are over.

What booking options does Amazon Relay offer during peak season?

Amazon names the load board, Post A Truck, short-term contracts, contract auctions and its equipment marketplace for renting trailers. It also offers load alerts for loads that meet your criteria.
