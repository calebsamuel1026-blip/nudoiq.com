Amazon Relay refresher: how an auto refresh extension works

Relay's load board reloads itself every 30 seconds. An Amazon Relay refresher reloads it on a timer you set, so new loads show up without you hitting reload.

NudoIQ · Published September 20, 2026 · Last updated October 16, 2026

Short answer

An Amazon Relay refresher is an extension that reloads the load board on a timer you set, so new loads show up without you clicking reload. Amazon says its board refreshes every 30 seconds. NudoIQ lets you set the speed.

What does an Amazon Relay refresher do?

Amazon says the load board auto-refreshes new spot freight matches every 30 seconds. Refresh by hand and you only see what's there the moment you click. A load that posts and books between clicks, you never see.

A refresher reloads the board for you, on a timer you set. You stop hitting reload, and you don't have to sit at the screen. Here's how the refresher and auto-booker extensions compare.

How fast should an Amazon Relay auto refresher run?

You pick. The trade is simple:

  • Shorter interval: you see new loads sooner, and your computer works harder.
  • Longer interval: easier on your laptop and battery, a bit later to see new loads.
  • Not watching the screen: use ALERT mode, so a sound plays on the page when a load clears your minimums.

Amazon doesn't publish a refresh limit for Relay. In NudoIQ you set it with the SPEED control on the bar and change it any time.

Alert, Search or AutoBook: which refresher mode?

NudoIQ's refresher has three modes. All three use your minimums: Min Net Profit, Min $/Mile and a New load minimum. Lane, equipment and dates come from your own Relay search.

ModeWhat it doesWhat you do
ALERTRefreshes and sounds an alert on the page when a load clears your minimumsDecide and book
SEARCHRefreshes quietly and floats new and price-up loads that clear your minimums to the topWatch and book
AUTOBOOKRefreshes and books loads that clear your Min Net Profit, up to your Max booksReview what it booked

AutoBook skips a load it can't fully price, and Max books runs from 1 to 20. Not sure yet? Start in ALERT and switch later.

How do you set up an Amazon Relay refresher?

  1. Add the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open Relay in the same Chrome profile and sign in as usual. The extension uses that session.
  3. Run your normal Relay search for the lane and equipment you want.
  4. Set your minimums and, for AutoBook, your Max books.
  5. Pick a mode and a speed, then press START.

Frequently asked questions

Is a short refresh interval too aggressive?

The shortest interval NudoIQ offers uses the most battery. If you're competing for loads on busy lanes, it helps you see new loads sooner. If you're not in a hurry, a longer interval is plenty.

Will my extension's refresh get me kicked off Relay?

Nobody can promise that, and we won't. The carrier is responsible for its Relay account and for following Relay's terms, so read them before you run any extension.

Do I need to leave my browser window open for the refresher to work?

Yes, the tab with the Relay load board needs to stay open. The refresher runs inside that tab. Close the browser or the tab and it stops.

Can I set different refresh intervals for different times of day?

You'd change it by hand. NudoIQ has no time-of-day schedule for refreshing, so move the speed control on the NudoIQ bar yourself.

Can a refresher book loads when I'm offline?

No. The refresher runs inside your browser. If your internet drops, it can't reload the board until you're back online.

Do I need to respond to an alert to book a load?

Not with AutoBook. It books loads that clear your Min Net Profit, up to your Max books, and you review what it booked. In ALERT mode you book when you hear the sound, and in SEARCH mode when you spot the floated load.

Is Post A Truck better than refreshing the Amazon Relay load board?

They do different jobs. With Post A Truck you post your availability and minimum rate per mile up to two weeks ahead, and matched carriers get first access to freight before it reaches the board. A refresher keeps the board itself current so you see new spot loads as they post. Here's how to post a truck on Amazon Relay.

What if I book a load off the refreshed board and then can't run it?

Cancelling a load you booked counts against your acceptance metric, and Amazon says the closer to pickup you reject, the higher the penalty. Letting it expire counts too. If you know it won't work, release it early. More in what happens if you cancel a load on Amazon Relay.
