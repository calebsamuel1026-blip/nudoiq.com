Best Amazon Relay Extensions: Refresher and Auto-Booker Tools

Several extensions can refresh the Relay load board and book loads for you. Here's what to look for when choosing one, and how the top tools compare.

Short answer

Why You Need a Relay Extension

Amazon says the Relay load board auto-refreshes new spot freight matches every 30 seconds, and Relay now shows its own auto-refresh control. Good loads can still go fast, and watching the board all day isn't practical if you're driving, managing dispatch or running your business. An extension reloads the board for you and alerts you or books loads by your rules, so you don't have to watch the screen.

Besides the bigger names, there are a dozen or so smaller refreshers in the category. Understanding what to look for helps you pick the right one.

What to Look For in a Relay Extension

Refresh Control: Can you set the interval (how often it reloads) yourself, and start or stop it right from the load board?

Booking Rules: Can you set rules to book only certain loads? Look for the ability to filter by lane, price range, load type, distance, and equipment. A tool that books only loads matching your rules books fewer loads you didn't want.

Profit Floor and Max-Books Cap: Does it let you set a minimum profit and a maximum number of books? These guardrails protect you from booking loads you didn't intend to take. A profit floor means "don't book anything below $X net profit." A max-books cap means "stop booking once I've booked 5 loads."

Rate Data and Decision Support: Does it show you lane history, net profit per load, or other rate context? Or does it just alert you when a load posts and let you decide? Tools that surface rate data help you make faster decisions.

Alerts: Can it alert you when a load matching your rules posts? NudoIQ sounds an alert on the page and sends a Telegram message to your phone if you connect Telegram.

Data Handling and Privacy: Where does your data go? Some tools store booking rules on your computer only. Others upload to a server. Ask yourself: do I want my lane preferences, profit targets, and booking history on someone else's server?

Updates and Support: Is the tool actively maintained? An extension that hasn't been updated in months might break when Chrome or Relay changes. Look for tools with recent updates and a way to report problems.

Cost: Free tools exist. Paid tools add more features. Decide whether the features are worth the cost for your operation.

What Makes a Good Extension Choice

A good extension for you is one that:

  1. Automates what you do manually: If you refresh constantly, it should refresh for you. If you struggle to judge rates, it should show you rate history.
  1. Includes guardrails: Set a profit floor, set a max-books cap, and the tool respects those limits so you don't accidentally book unprofitable work.

  1. Fits your budget: Free is good if it does what you need. Paid is worth it if the extra features speed up your decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use two refresher extensions at the same time?

Technically you can, but it's not recommended. Two extensions both trying to refresh the board and book loads can cause conflicts, duplicate bookings, or unexpected behavior. Pick one extension and stick with it.

Do I need a separate account to use a Relay extension?

It depends on the extension, so check each one before you install. NudoIQ asks for your email address when you start the free trial, and you activate the extension with a code sent to that email. After that, it runs in your own Chrome browser, inside your signed-in Relay tab, and never asks for your Amazon password.

How fast do these extensions book loads?

An extension in Autobook mode books as soon as it sees a matching load on a refresh, without you reading and clicking. How soon it sees the load depends on your refresh interval. No tool can promise you'll get a load first.

What if an extension books a load I didn't want?

Set a profit floor and max-books cap. These are your primary defenses. A profit floor means the extension won't book loads that don't clear your minimum profit. A max-books cap means it stops booking once you've booked, say, 5 loads. Together, they prevent unwanted bookings.

Is NudoIQ's lane rate history always accurate?

The 90-day range in NudoIQ comes from the Relay load board's posted rates. The target rate is an estimate built on that history, not a guarantee, and the confidence level shows how much data is behind it. Amazon rates change based on season, time of day, volume, and other factors, so past rates don't guarantee today's rate. That's why NudoIQ calls it decision support: it helps you judge whether today's rate is high or low, but you still decide whether to take it.
