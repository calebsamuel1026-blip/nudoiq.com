Why You Need a Relay Extension

The Amazon Relay load board refreshes when you reload it. Good loads book within seconds. Manual refreshing every 30 seconds isn't practical if you're driving, managing dispatch, or running your business. An extension automates the reload and notifies you or books loads for you, so you don't have to watch the screen.

Besides the bigger names, there are a dozen or so smaller refreshers in the category. Understanding what to look for helps you pick the right one.

What to Look For in a Relay Extension

Refresh Control: Can you set the interval (how often it reloads) and does it add jitter (random variation to prevent server strain)? A good refresher lets you choose 1 to 5 second intervals and includes built-in jitter so every refresh isn't at the exact same moment.

Booking Rules: Can you set rules to book only certain loads? Look for the ability to filter by lane, price range, load type, distance, and equipment. A tool that books only loads matching your criteria is safer than one that books indiscriminately.

Profit Floor and Max-Books Cap: Does it let you set a minimum profit and a maximum number of books? These guardrails protect you from booking loads you didn't intend to take. A profit floor means "don't book anything below $X net profit." A max-books cap means "stop booking once I've booked 5 loads today."

Rate Data and Decision Support: Does it show you lane history, net profit per load, or other rate context? Or does it just notify you when a load posts and let you decide? Tools that surface rate data help you make faster decisions.

Alerts: Can it notify you when a load matching your rules posts? NudoIQ sends Telegram alerts, which reach your phone.

Data Handling and Privacy: Where does your data go? Some tools store booking rules on your computer only. Others upload to a server. Ask yourself: do I want my lane preferences, profit targets, and booking history on someone else's server?

Updates and Support: Is the tool actively maintained? An extension that hasn't been updated in months might break when Chrome or Relay changes. Look for tools with recent updates and a way to report problems.

Cost: Free tools exist. Paid tools add more features. Decide whether the features are worth the cost for your operation.

The Top Extensions

LoadFetcher is the largest Amazon Relay extension by installs. It offers a free refresher tier and is focused on refreshing, pricing history and alerts.

What's advertised: auto refresh, load pricing and history, backhaul search, Post Truck Manager, Telegram alerts and trip tracking. What's not listed: automatic booking, net profit per load, or scorecard dispute letters (checked October 4, 2026).

LoadFetcher's pitch is simplicity and cost: a free refresher tier. If that's all you need, the free tier may be enough.

Rocket Relay and Rocket Pro

Rocket Relay is a refresher and booker with a free tier. Its core pitch is that your business data stays on your own computer. It's focused on refreshing and booking.

Best for: Carriers who want a free refresher and booker and prioritize keeping their data local.

What Makes a Good Extension Choice

A good extension for you is one that:

  1. Automates what you do manually: If you refresh constantly, it should refresh for you. If you struggle to judge rates, it should show you rate history.
  1. Includes guardrails: Set a profit floor, set a max-books cap, and the tool respects those limits so you don't accidentally book unprofitable work.
  1. Stays updated: Recent updates mean the developers are actively maintaining it and fixing issues.
  1. Respects your data: Whether it stores locally or on a server, you should know what happens to your booking preferences and history.
  1. Fits your budget: Free is good if it does what you need. Paid is worth it if the extra features speed up your decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use two refresher extensions at the same time?

Technically you can, but it's not recommended. Two extensions both trying to refresh the board and book loads can cause conflicts, duplicate bookings, or unexpected behavior. Pick one extension and stick with it.

Do I need a separate account to use a Relay extension?

It depends on the extension, so check each one before you install. NudoIQ asks for your email address when you start the free trial, and you activate the extension with a code sent to that email. After that, it runs in your own Chrome browser, inside your signed-in Relay tab, and never asks for your Amazon password.

How fast do these extensions book loads?

An extension in Autobook mode books as soon as it sees a matching load on a refresh, without you reading and clicking. How soon it sees the load depends on your refresh interval. No tool can promise you'll get a load first.

What if an extension books a load I didn't want?

Set a profit floor and max-books cap. These are your primary defenses. A profit floor means the extension won't book loads that don't clear your minimum profit. A max-books cap means it stops booking once you've booked, say, 5 loads. Together, they prevent unwanted bookings.

Is NudoIQ's lane rate history always accurate?

Lane rate history in NudoIQ comes from the Relay load board's posted rates over 90 days. It's historical fact, not a prediction. But Amazon rates change based on season, time of day, volume, and other factors, so past rates don't guarantee today's rate. That's why NudoIQ calls it decision support: it helps you judge whether today's rate is high or low, but you still decide whether to take it.
