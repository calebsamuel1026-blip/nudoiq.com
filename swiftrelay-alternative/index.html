What is SwiftRelay?

SwiftRelay is a Chrome extension for the Amazon Relay load board, published by Premier IT Solutions. Its site names carriers and dispatch teams, and its listing names dispatchers. As of October 9, 2026, its site and listing name refresh, search, load highlights, one-click booking and filters. Pro and Enterprise add auto-book. The listing also names Competitor Insights, live load stats, Google Maps routes and multi-tab support.

Does Swift Relay auto book loads?

Yes, SwiftRelay auto books loads on its Pro and Enterprise plans. As of October 9, 2026, its FAQ says the Lite plan does not auto-book. Lite refreshes, searches and highlights loads. Pro is $74.95 a month and Enterprise is $349. NudoIQ's AutoBook books for you in one $49.99 plan, and only loads that clear your minimum net profit, after the costs you enter.

What does NudoIQ do that Swift Relay doesn't list?

SwiftRelay's listing and checked site pages didn't name these three: an AutoBook net profit floor, net profit per load and scorecard dispute letters.

  • Net profit floor for AutoBook: it books only loads that clear your minimum. It skips any load it can't fully price and stops at your max-books cap.
  • Net profit on every load: net dollars and $/mile next to the price. It uses the costs you enter, like fuel, MPG, driver pay and maintenance.
  • Scorecard dispute letters: it drafts the letter from the violation. You read it, send it, and Amazon makes the call.
  • Also: Spanish, Lane Intelligence (90-day lane price range), backhaul search and One-Click Post A Truck. Plus alerts or AutoBook on short-term contracts, and Telegram alerts to your phone.

What does Swift Relay do that NudoIQ doesn't?

As of October 9, 2026, SwiftRelay lists Competitor Insights, which shows loads booked by competitors on the load board. Its Pro plan adds auto-book filters for max stops, excluded states and stem time. Its Enterprise plan adds Speed Optimizer and one virtual machine. NudoIQ has none of those. SwiftRelay also sells plans by device count, from 2 to 10 at once, and lists multi-tab and multi-window support.

How much does Swift Relay cost vs NudoIQ?

On SwiftRelay, your choice is Lite at $49.95 a month, Pro at $74.95 or Enterprise at $349. Auto-book starts on Pro (checked October 9, 2026). NudoIQ starts with a free preview on install, then a 7-day free trial with no card needed. After that it's $49.99 a month flat for every truck. Cancel anytime.

Should you pick Swift Relay or NudoIQ?

Pick SwiftRelay if

  • You want to see loads booked by competitors on your load board.
  • You want auto-book filters for max stops, excluded states and stem time.
  • You run a dispatch team and want plans sized by device count, up to 10 devices.
  • You want the Enterprise plan, which adds one virtual machine.

Pick NudoIQ if

  • You want loads booked only when they clear your minimum net profit, after your own costs.
  • You want auto booking in one $49.99 plan for every truck.
  • You want lane prices, backhaul search and scorecard dispute letters in one extension.
  • You work in Spanish, or want Telegram load alerts on your phone.
