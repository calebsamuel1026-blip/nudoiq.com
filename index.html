Inside NudoIQ

Eleven tools, one extension. Click through to see them.

Each tab is a different tool built into NudoIQ. Click one to see exactly what it does.

Every lane shows a target rate, a ceiling, and a confidence level.

NudoIQ checks the posted price against that lane's history. It shows your target rate, the expected range, and how much data backs each estimate.

  • Target a number instead of guessing
  • See how much data supports each estimate
  • Open the breakdown when the call is close

See what a load actually pays before you touch the offer button.

NudoIQ stamps a net profit figure on every card on the load board. It takes the posted rate and subtracts driver pay, fuel, maintenance, and the deadhead miles to the pickup, then shows the dollars and the net rate per mile that are actually left.

  • See the real number, not the posted rate
  • Set a profit floor and hide loads under it
  • Stop an alert or autobook from firing on a loser

Turn an Amazon Relay scorecard hit into a filed dispute.

NudoIQ reads your scorecard, pulls out the violation behind the score, and drafts a dispute letter citing Amazon policy. You review it, add context, and submit.

  • Find the violation without hunting for it
  • File the same day instead of letting it stand
  • Add what happened in your own words

Know what your fleet earned this week, not next month.

Revenue, miles, fuel cost, net margin, driver ranking and trip detail land in one view, updated as trips complete.

  • See net margin without a reconciliation
  • Compare drivers and weeks side by side
  • Import CSV and pull settlement reports

Build a driver pay statement from completed trips.

NudoIQ totals the loads, miles and pay for the period and produces a statement you can print, save as PDF, or email to the driver.

  • Pay drivers from trip data, not a spreadsheet
  • Give drivers totals they can check line by line
  • Send it without retyping a single trip

Set a refresh speed and let NudoIQ keep the board moving.

A slider sets the refresh interval from 1 to 5 seconds. NudoIQ adds up to 20% random jitter to each interval so requests don't land on the same beat every time, and keeps the load board tab active.

  • Set your own refresh speed, 1 to 5 seconds
  • Random jitter up to 20% on every interval
  • Keeps the load board tab from going idle

Set your rules once, then let the control bar act.

The control bar has three modes: Alert pings you on a match, Search highlights matches, and Autobook books one for you. Set price and lane rules, a profit floor, and a booking cap, then get a Telegram alert when one lands.

  • Alert, search, or autobook: you pick the mode
  • Set a minimum profit floor before it books
  • Get a Telegram alert the moment one lands

Rank backhaul loads by pay, by rate, and by miles from home.

NudoIQ searches backhaul loads from where your current load ends, aimed back toward home within your mileage radius and day window. Sort by total pay, rate per mile, pickup time, or distance from home. The all-in rate counts the deadhead.

  • See which load is marked BEST OVERALL
  • Sort by pay, rate, pickup time, or distance home
  • Every rate counts the deadhead miles to reach it

See origin, destination, and price before you post a truck.

A modal over Post A Truck shows origin, destination, distance, and the board price next to your minimum dollars per mile and minimum payout. One button posts the truck, then shows Posting and Posted.

  • See distance and price before you commit
  • Compare the board price to your minimum payout
  • Post the truck with a single button

Book a load in one action instead of several steps.

Instant Book replaces the normal multi-step booking flow with a single action from the control bar. Click it once on a load you want and NudoIQ carries the booking through without the extra confirmation screens.

  • One click instead of the normal booking flow
  • Available right from the control bar
  • Skip the extra confirmation screens

Open the actual routed trip before you trust the mileage.

An icon on each load card opens the full routed trip in Google Maps, including every stop on a multi-stop run. You see the real driving miles instead of only the number the board states.

  • Open the route in Google Maps, one click
  • See every stop on a multi-stop run
  • Check real miles against the board's number

How Relay prices loads

We figured out how Relay decides what your load is worth.

Relay bases your rate on the same things Amazon looks at: season, time of day, origin, equipment, what booked before, and today's volume. NudoIQ tracks all six and ranks your posted rate against that lane's 90-day history.

Answers before you install.

Do I need to leave my computer on?

Yes. NudoIQ runs in your browser, so the Relay tab and computer need to remain available while it watches the board. Alerts help you step away without staring at the screen.

Does NudoIQ guarantee a rate or booking?

No. Rates, availability, and booking outcomes change constantly. NudoIQ provides alerts, historical context, estimates, and workflow tools to support your decision.

Is NudoIQ affiliated with Amazon?

No. NudoIQ is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.com, Inc. Carriers remain responsible for their accounts and compliance with applicable terms.

Which browsers are supported?

NudoIQ is built for Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers. Feature behavior can vary by browser version.

How do I cancel?

Go to nudoiq.com/billing and open your Stripe billing portal (Stripe will send a one-time code to your subscription email). Or email contact@nudoiq.com. Cancel during the 7-day trial with no charge; cancel later and the next renewal is stopped.

How can I get help?

Email us at contact@nudoiq.com or message NudoIQ support on Telegram.

What is the Amazon Relay load board?

It is the board inside Amazon Relay where Amazon posts freight to contracted carriers. Carriers search it, book loads, and post available trucks against it. NudoIQ does not replace that board. It runs inside it and adds rate context, alerts and reporting to the screens you already use.

Does NudoIQ see my Amazon Relay password?

NudoIQ runs inside your signed-in Relay browser tab. It never asks for your Amazon password or stores it. Your subscription email, driver information, and trip details are stored only for your account.

Will NudoIQ get my account suspended?

NudoIQ runs only in your own browser on Relay pages you're signed in to. It doesn't log in from another location or ask for your password. You control the refresh speed. Amazon's decisions about accounts are theirs alone, but NudoIQ stays within the Relay interface you already use.

Does NudoIQ work on load boards other than Amazon Relay?

No. NudoIQ is built specifically for the Amazon Relay load board and reads only Relay screens. It does not connect to DAT, Truckstop or other load boards.
