FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does NudoIQ work with my carrier grade?

Yes. NudoIQ works for every Amazon Relay carrier grade — from owner-operators just getting started to multi-truck fleets and brokerages. The features automatically adapt to what your Relay account has access to.

Is it safe? Will Amazon flag my account?

NudoIQ uses randomized refresh timing (just like a human browsing) and only interacts with the public Relay UI — it never touches Amazon's private API or bypasses any security. We've had 450+ carriers using it for over a year with zero bans. That said, your account is your responsibility, and we recommend reading Amazon's Terms of Service.

Do I need to leave my computer on?

Yes — NudoIQ runs in your browser, so it needs the tab open to watch the load board. Most carriers leave a dedicated laptop or desktop running. Telegram alerts mean you can step away and still get notified the moment a load matches.

What happens after my 7-day free trial?

If you love it, you'll be billed $49.99/month — that's it. If you don't, cancel before the trial ends and you're never charged. 96% of carriers who try it keep it.

How do I cancel?

Click "Manage subscription" in the extension settings, then "Cancel". Two clicks, no questions, no retention calls, no five-step forms. We mean it.

Which browsers does it support?

NudoIQ works on Chrome, Edge, Brave, and any Chromium-based browser. Firefox support is on the roadmap. You can install it on up to 3 devices on a single subscription.

How do I get support?

Email contact@nudoiq.com or message us on Telegram. We typically reply within a couple hours — usually faster because the team is small and obsessive about this product.
