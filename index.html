FAQ

Clear answers before you install.

Do I need to leave my computer on?

Yes. NudoIQ runs in your browser, so the Relay tab and computer need to remain available while it watches the board. Alerts help you step away without staring at the screen.

Does NudoIQ guarantee a rate or booking?

No. Rates, availability, and booking outcomes change constantly. NudoIQ provides alerts, historical context, estimates, and workflow tools to support your decision.

Is NudoIQ affiliated with Amazon?

No. NudoIQ is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.com, Inc. Carriers remain responsible for their accounts and compliance with applicable terms.

Which browsers are supported?

NudoIQ is built for Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers. Feature behavior can vary by browser version.

How do I cancel?

Open the extension settings and choose Manage subscription. You can cancel before the trial ends or stop a future renewal.

Email us at contact@nudoiq.com or message NudoIQ support on Telegram.
