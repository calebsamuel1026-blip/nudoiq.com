What is Rocket Relay?

Rocket Relay is a browser extension for the Amazon Relay load board, from West Wanted. Its store listing is called Relay Auto Booker Refresher Rocket Pro. As of October 9, 2026, its listing and site name Alert, Search and Auto-book modes and one-click FastBook. They also name filters, bulk Post-a-Truck, price-change alerts and custom sounds. It runs in Chrome, Edge and Brave, and on Android in Lemur or Quetta.

Does Rocket Relay auto book loads?

Yes, Rocket Relay auto books loads on its Pro plan. As of October 9, 2026, its listing says Auto-book takes matching loads by your filters, best-paying first. It stops at the number you set and runs only after you press Start. NudoIQ's AutoBook books for you too, but only loads that clear your minimum net profit, after the costs you enter.

What does NudoIQ do that Rocket Relay doesn't list?

Rocket Relay's listing and checked site pages didn't name these three: an AutoBook net profit floor, net profit per load and scorecard dispute letters.

  • Net profit floor for AutoBook: it books only loads that clear your minimum. It skips any load it can't fully price and stops at your max-books cap.
  • Net profit on every load: net dollars and $/mile next to the price. It uses the costs you enter, like fuel, MPG, driver pay and maintenance.
  • Scorecard dispute letters: it drafts the letter from the violation. You read it, send it, and Amazon makes the call.
  • Also: Lane Intelligence (90-day lane price range), backhaul search, alerts or AutoBook on short-term contract offers, and Telegram alerts to your phone.

What does Rocket Relay do that NudoIQ doesn't?

As of October 9, 2026, Rocket Relay's own pages list these, and NudoIQ doesn't have them.

  • A Free plan you can stay on, with FastBook on matched trips and no card required. NudoIQ's free preview also refreshes and alerts, but it has no booking and ends when you start the trial.
  • More booking rules (Pro): location by state, ZIP or facility, distance, stops, deadhead, stem time and time windows. It can watch several lanes at once.
  • Bulk Post-a-Truck (Pro): modify, clone or delete many orders at once and assign drivers. Pro also puts FastBook on every trip.
  • More languages for your board: its listing names 10, from German to Ukrainian.
  • Local data: its listing says your loads, trips, payouts and drivers stay on your computer. NudoIQ keeps your driver list on a trial or paid plan, and any Fleet Dashboard trips, on its servers (privacy policy).
  • Also listed: a ~ key to start and stop and your own alert sounds (Free). Pro adds auto-expanded trip stops, trailer-status badges and a filter for loads at Amazon locations only.

How much does Rocket Relay cost vs NudoIQ?

On Rocket Relay, you pick a Free plan or Pro at $49 a month (checked October 9, 2026). Its 7-day Pro trial takes a card, but you aren't charged during it. NudoIQ starts with a free preview on install, then a 7-day free trial with no card needed. After that it's $49.99 a month flat for every truck. Cancel anytime.

Should you pick Rocket Relay or NudoIQ?

Pick Rocket Relay if

  • You want to stay on a free plan. Its Free plan has auto-refresh, alerts and FastBook on matched trips.
  • You want auto booking by location, distance, stops or time windows, or on several lanes at once.
  • You post many trucks and want to change Post-a-Truck orders in bulk.
  • You want your load data kept on your own computer, or need a language beyond English and Spanish.

Pick NudoIQ if

  • You want loads booked only when they clear your minimum net profit, after your own costs.
  • You want to see what each load nets you, and what its lane has paid, before you take it.
  • You fight scorecard hits and want the dispute letter drafted for you.
  • You want 7 days of auto booking with no card, or Telegram load alerts on your phone.

How does NudoIQ decide which loads to book?

In AutoBook mode, NudoIQ books only loads that clear your minimum net profit. If you set a minimum $/mile, they must clear that too. It never books a load it can't fully price, such as one missing a pickup time, miles or pay. It stops at your max-books cap. ALERT mode tells you about a match without booking it. That matters, because cancelling a booked load counts against you.
