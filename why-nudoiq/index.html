Why do carriers choose NudoIQ over other Amazon Relay extensions?

The facts side by side: what NudoIQ does, what only NudoIQ does, and what other Relay extensions list.

NudoIQ · Published October 8, 2026 · Last updated October 8, 2026

Short answer

NudoIQ is the only Amazon Relay extension that books loads to your own minimum net profit and drafts scorecard dispute letters (checked October 5, 2026, across 52 other Relay listings on the Chrome Web Store). It is also the only extension in our side-by-side that lists all six core features, from auto-book to Telegram alerts. Every feature costs one flat $49.99 a month, the same for one truck or twenty.

Why carriers choose NudoIQ

  • AutoBook books only loads that clear your own Min Net Profit, and skips a load it cannot fully price.
  • Every load card shows net profit and net $/mile after driver pay, fuel, maintenance and deadhead to pickup.
  • NudoIQ drafts scorecard dispute letters from the violation. You review and submit the letter. Amazon decides.
  • Lane Intelligence shows 90 days of a lane's prices, a target rate, an expected range and a confidence level. It is decision support, not a promise of a rate.
  • The refresher runs every 1 to 5 seconds with jitter, in Alert, Search or AutoBook mode, with a profit floor and a max-books cap.
  • One flat price: $49.99 a month for every feature, the same for one truck or twenty. 7-day free trial, no card needed to start.
  • The interface is in English and Spanish.
  • It runs in your own signed-in Relay tab and never asks for your Amazon password.

NudoIQ also includes Instant Book, Telegram alerts, a backhaul finder and one-click Post A Truck with a recommended bid. The recommended bid improves with use, so it is not accurate right after install.

A free preview starts on install: refresh, alerts, the NudoIQ bar and Lane Intelligence on the first 5 priced loads a day. AutoBook, Instant Book and dispute letters come with the 7-day free trial.

What only NudoIQ does

On October 5, 2026, we checked 52 other Amazon Relay listings on the Chrome Web Store. Two features are on NudoIQ and on none of them:

  1. Scorecard dispute letters. No other Relay listing offers them. NudoIQ drafts the letter from the violation, you submit it, and Amazon makes the call.
  2. AutoBook gated by net profit after your own costs. Other extensions filter by payout, $/mile, stops or deadhead. None of them books to a net number. NudoIQ books a load only when what you keep, after driver pay, fuel, maintenance and deadhead, clears the floor you set.

What other extensions list that NudoIQ doesn't

  • Booking on a free plan. Rocket Pro's free tier lists FastBook on matches. NudoIQ's free preview covers refresh, alerts and lane prices; booking comes with the 7-day free trial, then $49.99 a month.
  • Multi-load booking. RGD and SwiftRelay list booking more than one load at a time. NudoIQ does not.
  • Trip and driver tracking. LoadFetcher and EasyRelay offer trip and driver tracking. NudoIQ does not.
  • Annual plan. NudoIQ has no annual plan. It is month to month.

Who NudoIQ fits, and who it doesn't

NudoIQ fits you if you want AutoBook to take only loads that clear your own net number, you want to see what a load nets before you take it, you deal with scorecard violations, or you run more than one truck and want one flat price.

Another tool fits you better if you want to book loads on a free plan, you want to book several loads in one click, or you need trip and driver tracking in the same tool.
