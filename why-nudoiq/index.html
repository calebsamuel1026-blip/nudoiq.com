Why carriers choose NudoIQ

  • AutoBook books only loads that clear your own Min Net Profit, and skips a load it cannot fully price.
  • Every load card shows net profit and net $/mile after driver pay, fuel, maintenance and deadhead to pickup.
  • NudoIQ drafts scorecard dispute letters from the violation. You review and submit the letter. Amazon decides.
  • Lane Intelligence shows 90 days of a lane's prices, a target rate, an expected range and a confidence level. It is decision support, not a promise of a rate.
  • The auto refresher runs in Alert, Search or AutoBook mode, with a profit floor and a max-books cap.
  • One flat price: $49.99 a month for every feature, the same for one truck or twenty. 7-day free trial, no card needed to start.
  • The interface is in English and Spanish.
  • It runs in your own signed-in Relay tab and never asks for your Amazon password.

NudoIQ also includes Instant Book, Telegram alerts, a backhaul finder and one-click Post A Truck with a recommended bid. The recommended bid improves with use, so it is not accurate right after install.

What only NudoIQ does

On October 5, 2026, we checked 52 other Amazon Relay listings on the Chrome Web Store. Two features are on NudoIQ and on none of them:

  1. Scorecard dispute letters. No other Relay listing offers them. NudoIQ drafts the letter from the violation, you submit it, and Amazon makes the call.
  2. AutoBook gated by net profit after your own costs. Other extensions filter by payout, $/mile, stops or deadhead. None of them books to a net number. NudoIQ books a load only when what you keep, after driver pay, fuel, maintenance and deadhead, clears the floor you set.
