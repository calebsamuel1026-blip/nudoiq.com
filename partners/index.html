FAQ

What is NudoIQ exactly?

A Chrome extension that runs on the Amazon Relay board. It auto-refreshes the board every few seconds. It shows what each load has paid before. It can book loads automatically when they match your rules. It tracks every trip's real profit, after fuel and driver pay.

Who are the carriers using it?

Owner-operators and dispatchers who book loads on Amazon Relay. The rating is 5.0 on the Chrome Web Store.

What if my audience doesn't convert?

You still owe us nothing. You get paid only when someone subscribes with your code and completes their first month. If they cancel before payment, no commission. That is the deal.

How long until I see a payout?

First commission arrives after the first subscriber's monthly payment clears. We pay to the email you sign up with.
