FAQ

What is NudoIQ exactly?

A Chrome extension that runs on the Amazon Relay board. It refreshes the board for you, at a speed you pick. It shows what a lane has paid before. It can book loads that match your rules and clear your minimum net profit. It drafts scorecard dispute letters. It tracks every trip's net profit, after fuel and driver pay. Independent software, not affiliated with Amazon.

Who are the carriers using it?

Owner-operators and dispatchers who book loads on Amazon Relay. The rating is 5.0 on the Chrome Web Store.

What do I owe you if nobody signs up?

You owe us nothing. You get paid only when a carrier you sent pays. If a carrier cancels, your half stops with their last payment.

How long until I see a payout?

We pay monthly. Each month we add up what your carriers paid and send you half, by a method we agree on when you join, like PayPal or bank transfer.

How long do I keep getting paid?

For as long as that carrier stays subscribed. That holds even if you stop posting.

Do you pay a flat fee per video?

No. You get 50% of what your carriers pay, and nothing per video. There's no minimum number of posts either.
