What is RGD?

RGD is a Chrome extension for the Amazon Relay load board, from Rolling Global Digital, per its website checked October 9, 2026.

Does RGD auto book loads?

Yes, on both its plans, by the filters you select (checked October 9, 2026). Its listing and site pages name no net profit setting. NudoIQ's AutoBook books only loads that clear your minimum net profit.

What does NudoIQ do for your loads that RGD doesn't list?

Three things, none on RGD's listing or website as of October 9, 2026.

  • Net profit floor: AutoBook books only loads that clear your Min Net Profit.
  • Net profit on every load: net dollars and $/mile after the costs you enter.
  • Scorecard dispute letters: drafted for you. You review and send. Amazon decides.
