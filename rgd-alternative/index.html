What is RGD?

RGD is a Chrome extension for Amazon Relay carriers, dispatchers and fleet owners, from Rolling Global Digital. Its store listing names Multi Booking, advanced filters, a Board Intelligence Mode and a Related Loads block (checked October 9, 2026). Its website lists desktop notifications on both plans, and contracts on the Business plan. The listing also names nine Relay regions, including the US, Canada and the UK.

Does RGD auto book loads?

Yes, RGD auto books loads based on the filters you select. Its Multi Booking can take 2, 3, 5 or even 10 trips in one click. RGD can stop at the confirmation step so you can check the load. On October 9, 2026, its listing and checked site pages named no net profit or cost setting. NudoIQ's AutoBook books for you, and only loads that clear your minimum net profit.

What does NudoIQ do for your loads that RGD doesn't list?

Three things RGD's listing and checked site pages don't name: a net profit floor for AutoBook, net profit per load, and scorecard dispute letters.

  • Net profit floor for AutoBook: it books only loads that clear your minimum. It skips any load it can't fully price and stops at your max-books cap.
  • Net profit on every load: net dollars and $/mile next to the price. It uses the costs you enter, like fuel, MPG, driver pay and maintenance.
  • Scorecard dispute letters: it drafts the letter from the violation. You read it, send it, and Amazon makes the call.
  • Also: Telegram alerts to your phone, Spanish, Lane Intelligence (90-day lane price range), backhaul search and One-Click Post A Truck.

What does RGD do for your loads that NudoIQ doesn't?

As of October 9, 2026, RGD lists five things for your board that NudoIQ doesn't have. One smaller difference: RGD's free trial starts with no account, while NudoIQ's asks for an email.

  • Several loads in one click: its Multi Booking books 2, 3, 5 or even 10 trips with a single click. It also lists multi-load search and identical-trip series.
  • Desktop notifications on your computer: both RGD plans list them, single or multiple.
  • A stop at the confirm step: RGD can pause at the confirmation stage so you can check the chosen load first.
  • More filters: its listing names custom locations, time ranges and load types. Its plan table puts Time Frame and Exclude Locations on Business ($249) only, and no filters on Essential.
  • Board extras: a Related Loads block shows more trips while your main search is loading. It also alerts you when selected trips become unavailable.

How much does RGD cost you vs NudoIQ?

On RGD, you choose Essential at $79 a month or Business at $249 a month. Business adds contracts and more filters. Both are billed monthly (checked October 9, 2026). NudoIQ starts with a free preview on install, then a 7-day free trial with no card needed. After that it's $49.99 a month flat for every truck. Cancel anytime.

Should you pick RGD or NudoIQ?

Pick RGD if

  • You want to book 2 to 10 trips with one click, or run identical trips for drivers on similar loads.
  • You want desktop notifications on your computer.
  • You want it to stop at the confirmation step so you can check each load first.
  • You filter by custom locations, time ranges or load types, or want Business ($249) for Time Frame and Exclude Locations.

Pick NudoIQ if

  • You want loads booked only when they clear your minimum net profit, after your own costs.
  • You want to see what each load nets you before you take it.
  • You fight scorecard hits and want the dispute letter drafted for you.
  • You want auto booking for $49.99 a month, or you work in Spanish.
