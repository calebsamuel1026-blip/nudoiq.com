Privacy Policy

NudoIQ Chrome Extension · Last updated: May 16, 2026

NudoIQ ("we", "our", or "us") operates the NudoIQ Chrome extension. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

  • Email address — collected when you activate your subscription. Used solely to verify your license status against our database.
  • Load board data — anonymized pricing observations ($/mile, distance, origin state, destination state) from Amazon Relay loads you view. No personal information, driver names, or load IDs are stored. This data is used to build shared market intelligence for all users.
  • Settings — your extension preferences (MPG, diesel price, alert thresholds) are stored locally in Chrome sync storage and never transmitted to our servers.
  • Telegram credentials — your bot token and chat ID are stored locally in Chrome sync storage only. We never transmit or store these on our servers.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your Amazon credentials, passwords, or account information.
  • We do not collect driver names, addresses, or personally identifiable information from load cards.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

How we use your data

  • License verification — your email is checked against our Supabase database to confirm an active subscription.
  • Market intelligence — anonymized load pricing observations are pooled across users to provide historical rate data for Amazon Relay lanes. No individual user's data is ever exposed or attributed.

Data storage

License and subscription data is stored in Supabase (supabase.com), hosted in the United States. Subscription billing is handled by Stripe (stripe.com). We do not store payment card information.

Data retention

License records are retained while your subscription is active and for 90 days after cancellation. Anonymized load observations are retained for 90 days on a rolling basis.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your account data at any time by emailing contact@nudoiq.com. We will delete your email and license record within 7 business days.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — database hosting (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Anthropic — AI dispute letter generation; only the scorecard text you explicitly submit is sent (anthropic.com/privacy)
  • Telegram — optional alert notifications using credentials you provide (telegram.org/privacy)

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.
