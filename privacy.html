Privacy Policy

NudoIQ Chrome Extension & Website  ·  Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

NudoIQ ("we", "our", or "us") operates the NudoIQ Chrome extension and the nudoiq.com website. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user. Most of this policy describes the extension; the Website Analytics & Advertising section below describes the separate tracking that runs on our website pages.

What We Collect

  • Email address Collected when you activate your subscription. Used solely to verify your license status against our database.
  • Load board observations Anonymized pricing data (rate per mile, distance, origin state, destination state) from Amazon Relay loads you view. Load IDs are stored as anonymous identifiers to deduplicate observations — they are not linked to your account or any personally identifiable information. This data is used to build shared market intelligence for all users.
  • Fleet trip data If you use the Fleet Dashboard feature, completed trip records (origin, destination, miles, revenue, dates) and driver names are synced to our Supabase database to power your fleet analytics. This data is visible only to you and is never shared with other users or third parties.
  • Extension settings Your preferences (MPG, diesel price, alert thresholds, etc.) are stored locally in Chrome sync storage and never transmitted to our servers.
  • Subscription usage activity While you hold a paid or trial subscription we record when the extension is used: your subscription email, the type of action (for example a dispute letter generated or a load auto-booked), a random per-installation identifier, the time, and the IP address the request came from. We keep this to prove a subscription was genuinely used if a payment is disputed with a bank or card network, and to detect one subscription being shared across many machines. It is never sold, never shared with other users, and is not used for advertising. Records older than 400 days are deleted.
  • Telegram credentials Your bot token is stored in Chrome local storage on the machine you entered it on, so it does not replicate to your other Chrome installations. Your chat ID is stored in Chrome sync storage. We never store either on our servers; the token is sent only to Telegram, to deliver your alerts.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Amazon credentials, passwords, or account information
  • Addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information from load cards
  • Browsing history outside of relay.amazon.com
  • Payment card information (handled entirely by Stripe)
  • We do not sell your data to any third party
  • We do not use anything the extension collects — your email, load observations, fleet data, or settings — for advertising, and we never send it to advertising networks

Website Analytics & Advertising

This section covers the nudoiq.com website only. None of the tracking below runs inside the Chrome extension, and none of it can see your Amazon Relay activity, your load data, or your fleet data.

  • Meta Pixel (Facebook & Instagram advertising) Our home page at nudoiq.com and its Spanish version at nudoiq.com/es load the Meta Pixel (dataset ID 1601820741955520), a tracking script from Meta Platforms. When it loads it sends Meta a page-view event together with your IP address, your browser and device information (user agent), the page address you are on, and the page that referred you. It also sets a cookie named _fbp in your browser, which holds a random identifier Meta uses to recognize the same browser across visits. Separately, when you arrive from one of our ads the link carries a click identifier in the address bar (fbclid); our page saves that value in your browser’s local storage under the keys nudoiq_fbc and nudoiq_fbc_ts so that if you install and activate NudoIQ later, that sign-up can be attributed back to the ad you clicked. We use all of this for advertising measurement — to see which ads lead to installs and subscriptions — and for showing our ads to people on Meta’s platforms. Meta processes this data as an independent controller under its own terms. See Meta’s Privacy Policy and Meta’s ad settings, where you can limit how your activity off Meta is used.
  • Microsoft Clarity (website analytics) All pages on nudoiq.com, including this one, load Microsoft Clarity (tag y9n5ejt2k9). Clarity records how visitors use the site — page views, clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, plus your IP address, browser, device and approximate location — and replays it to us as anonymized session recordings and heatmaps so we can improve the pages. It sets its own cookies for this. We use Clarity for site improvement, not for advertising. See Microsoft’s Privacy Statement.

How to opt out: browser-level tracking protection (Firefox, Brave, Safari), an ad or script blocker, or Chrome’s third-party cookie blocking will stop these scripts from loading or from setting cookies. Nothing on this site depends on them, and the extension works normally either way. You can also clear the nudoiq_fbc values at any time by clearing site data for nudoiq.com in your browser settings.

How We Use Your Data

License verification — your email is checked against our Supabase database to confirm an active subscription.

Market intelligence — anonymized load pricing observations are pooled across users to provide historical rate data for Amazon Relay lanes. No individual user's data is ever exposed or attributed to a specific user.

Fleet analytics — if you use the Fleet Dashboard, trip and driver data you enter is stored in your account and used exclusively to generate your fleet reports and driver statements.

Diesel prices — your approximate region (detected via reverse geolocation) is used to fetch local diesel prices from iExitApp. Your exact location is never stored.

Data Storage & Retention

License and subscription data is stored in Supabase (supabase.com), hosted in the United States. Subscription billing is handled by Stripe (stripe.com). We do not store payment card information.

Fleet trip and driver data is stored in Supabase, accessible only under your account credentials.

Retention periods:

License records are retained while your subscription is active and for 90 days after cancellation. Anonymized load observations are retained for 90 days on a rolling basis. Fleet trip records are retained until you request deletion.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account data at any time by emailing contact@nudoiq.com. We will delete your email, license record, and any fleet data within 7 business days.
