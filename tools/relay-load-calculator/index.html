How your net is worked out

Start with what the load pays. Then take off what the whole trip costs you, empty miles included. Your truck burns fuel on the way to the pickup and on the way home, so total miles are loaded miles plus deadhead.

From there, the costs are:

  • Fuel: total miles divided by your MPG, times the fuel price.
  • Other costs: total miles times your other cost per mile.
  • Tolls for the trip.

Net is the pay minus those three. Net per loaded mile is that net divided by the loaded miles.

What the break-even rate tells you

Break-even is your total cost divided by the loaded miles. It is the lowest rate per loaded mile that covers your costs on this load. Below it, the load costs you money to run.

Use it before you book. Compare it with the rate on the board. When the two are close, one long wait at the dock or a detour can wipe out what is left. Set your own floor from your own numbers. See Amazon Relay rates for how pricing works.

Why net per mile beats the posted rate per mile

The posted rate per mile does not know your truck. It leaves out your fuel, your driver pay and the empty miles you drive to get to the pickup.

Two loads can post the same rate and leave you with very different money once deadhead is counted. Net per loaded mile puts every load on the same footing, so you compare what each one leaves you after costs. For what affects pay on Relay, read how much Amazon Relay pays.

Frequently asked questions

Does deadhead count in the net?

Deadhead counts. It goes into your total miles, so fuel and other costs cover the empty drive to the pickup and back home. Net per mile and break-even are divided by loaded miles, since those are the miles the load pays for.

What MPG should I use?

Use your truck's average over the last few fill-ups, loaded and empty together. The calculator starts at 9, but box trucks and tractors run very different numbers, so put in your own.

Is what you type saved or sent anywhere?

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The math runs in your browser on this page.

Does NudoIQ show this on the Relay load board?

On your Relay board, NudoIQ shows the net and net per mile under the price on every load card. It works from costs you set once in Settings and counts deadhead to the pickup and the drive home, but not tolls. It runs in Chrome on a computer, and the net shows without starting a trial.

What goes in other cost per mile?

Put in everything you pay per mile besides fuel: driver pay, maintenance, tires and insurance, each worked out per mile. If you pay something by the week, like insurance or a truck note, divide it by the miles your truck runs in a normal week.
