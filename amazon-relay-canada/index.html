Amazon Relay Canada: requirements, insurance and how to sign up

Amazon runs a separate Relay site for Canada at relay.amazon.ca. Here's what it asks of Canadian carriers, how that differs from the US, and what the page doesn't say.

NudoIQ · Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 14, 2026

Short answer

Amazon Relay Canada runs on relay.amazon.ca. To haul within Canada you need an active NSC number, and for cross-border work you also need a DOT number with interstate authority and a valid MC number. Insurance minimums are in CAD, with cargo coverage of at least $150,000, and Quebec operators also need CNESST registration. Amazon says Canadian applications are approved or rejected within 2 to 4 business days.

Is Amazon Relay available in Canada?

Yes. Amazon runs a Canadian Relay site at relay.amazon.ca. The US site points there with a banner: "Looking for Amazon Relay Canada? Go to relay.amazon.ca." Amazon's Conditions of Use page says use of Relay in Canada is governed by the Conditions of Use (Canada).

Most of this page comes from the relay.amazon.ca home page, which carries Amazon's Canada FAQ. Where we compare with the US or mention Relay Rewards, we cite Amazon's US pages and say so. A separate FAQ page on relay.amazon.ca asked for an Amazon sign-in when we checked on October 14, 2026, so we couldn't read it.

What do you need to haul for Amazon Relay in Canada?

Your authority depends on where you run:

  • Within Canada: "an Active NSC number."
  • Cross-border: "An active NSC number, DOT number with interstate authority, and a valid MC number."

The Canada page also lists a "Carrier" entity type that is authorized for property and for hire, and an FMCSA safety rating of Satisfactory, None or Not Rated. The US rules are in our Amazon Relay requirements guide.

What insurance does Amazon Relay Canada require?

The Canada page says "All Requirements in CAD." Most limits use the same figures as the US. Cargo doesn't.

Operating in Quebec adds one more step. The page says CNESST registration "is also required if your company operates in Quebec." Our insurance requirements guide explains each US coverage in more detail.

How long does Amazon Relay Canada approval take?

Days, not weeks. The Canada page says "your application will be approved or rejected within 2-4 business days." Like the US, it says any delay is due to validating insurance information. Amazon urges you to have all data ready before you apply.

Then: "Once approved, you will immediately be able to book loads from our load board." You also get access to the Relay portal's FAQs and operating guides.

For comparison, Amazon's US FAQ says 1 to 3 weeks.

What equipment and loads are on Amazon Relay Canada?

The Canada page lists:

  • Tractor: day cab, box truck and sleeper cab
  • Trailers: 53' dry van and 26' box truck

The Relay load board lets you "View and book spot work for dry vans and box trucks, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week." With Post A Truck, you share "when and where your trucks are available for up to two weeks in advance," and Relay matches you with loads that meet your origin, destination and rate.

The Canada page lists no reefer or intermodal equipment. That doesn't prove they're absent. Amazon just doesn't say.

How do pay and support work in Canada?

The same way as the US, per the Canada page:

  • Pay: "Payment cycle runs weekly from Sunday-Saturday." Everything completed before 23:59 Saturday is paid the following Friday.
  • Invoices: Relay generates them automatically in the portal.
  • Pay problems: submit a payment dispute in the portal within 30 days of reviewing your weekly invoice.
  • Load problems: the Relay Operations Center gives 24/7 support, reached "through the Relay portal or Relay mobile."

The Canada page gives no phone number.

What does Amazon expect from Canadian carriers?

The Canada page lists "Compliance with safety, NSC, DOT, MC and insurance requirements," on-time delivery, consistent tender acceptance, and running loads on Relay technology. It adds: "All carriers and drivers are required to use our Amazon Relay products for all Amazon loads."

Your performance shows on a scorecard in the Relay portal. The full expectations sit in the Carrier Terms of Service and Program Policies, which you see when you onboard.

Does Relay Rewards apply in Canada?

Amazon's pages point two ways. The Canada home page lists "Rewards and Discounts" with "Early access to loads and discounts on fuel, tires, maintenance, and more." But Amazon's Relay Rewards post says that program "is currently available only in the United States."

Amazon doesn't publish which discounts, if any, Canadian carriers get. Ask Relay once you're onboarded.

How do you sign up for Amazon Relay Canada?

Start on relay.amazon.ca with Get started. Have these ready first:

  1. Your active NSC number, plus your DOT and MC numbers if you'll run cross-border
  2. Insurance certificates that meet the CAD limits above
  3. CNESST registration if you operate in Quebec

Before your first booking, check what a load would leave you after costs. Our free Relay load calculator does that from a load's pay and distance.

What doesn't Amazon publish for Canada?

  • A Canadian phone number
  • Whether reefer or intermodal loads run in Canada
  • How much of the work is cross-border
  • Which provinces are served
  • Which discounts Canadian carriers get
  • Whether support is offered in French

Frequently asked questions

What are the Amazon Relay Canada requirements?

To haul within Canada, you need an active NSC number. For cross-border work you also need a DOT number with interstate authority and a valid MC number. Insurance is in CAD, including cargo coverage of at least $150,000, and Quebec operators need CNESST registration.

Is there an Amazon Relay Canada contact number?

Amazon doesn't publish a Relay phone number for Canada. The relay.amazon.ca page says the 24/7 Relay Operations Center is reached through the Relay portal or Relay mobile.

How do I sign up for Amazon Relay Canada?

Start with Get started on relay.amazon.ca. Have your NSC number, your DOT and MC numbers if you'll run cross-border, and insurance that meets the CAD minimums ready before you apply.

How long does Amazon Relay Canada take to approve an application?

The relay.amazon.ca page says applications are approved or rejected within 2 to 4 business days, with any delay due to insurance validation. Once approved, you can book from the load board right away.

Does Amazon Relay Canada have a load board?

Yes, Relay Canada has a load board. The relay.amazon.ca page says carriers can view and book spot work for dry vans and box trucks 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and use Post A Truck to share availability up to two weeks ahead.

Is Amazon Relay Canada cargo insurance different from the US?

Yes, your cargo limit is higher in Canada. The Canada page asks for cargo coverage of at least $150,000 CAD. Amazon's US FAQ asks for at least $100,000.
