Questions carriers actually ask

Do I have to move off the Relay board?

No. NudoIQ is a Chrome extension that renders inside the board you already use. There is no second app, no export, no separate login.

Does it work for one truck?

Yes, but it earns its keep fastest between about five and fifteen trucks, where the margin reporting and driver pay statements save real hours every week.

Is the bid target a guarantee?

No. It is an estimate with a stated confidence level, and NudoIQ tells you when the data behind it is thin. It is there to replace a guess with a better-informed guess, not to promise a booking.
