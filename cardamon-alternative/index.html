Cardamon Relay alternative FAQ

What is Cardamon Relay?

Cardamon is a Chrome extension for Amazon Relay, from Cardamon Inc. (checked October 16, 2026). NudoIQ is a separate one, with AutoBook at your minimum net profit.

Is Cardamon working?

Its newest written store review, from September 11, 2026, is 1 star: "Not proper work." Its listing was last updated October 4, 2026 (checked October 16, 2026). We did not test their product.

Does Cardamon auto book loads?

Yes, on its Pro plan, per its store listing (checked October 16, 2026). NudoIQ's AutoBook books only loads that clear your minimum net profit, and skips any load it can't fully price.

Does Cardamon show net profit per load?

Not on its listing or website (checked October 16, 2026). NudoIQ shows net dollars and $/mile on every load, after the costs you enter.

Is Cardamon a Chrome extension?

Yes, it installs from the Chrome Web Store. NudoIQ is a separate Chrome extension. If you try both, run one load board extension at a time so they don't clash.

Can I switch from Cardamon to NudoIQ?

Yes, in a few minutes. Turn off or remove Cardamon, add NudoIQ from the Chrome Web Store and start your 7 days free. Your Relay login stays the same.

Is NudoIQ or Cardamon made by Amazon?

No. NudoIQ is an independent product by Sami Enterprises LLC, not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Amazon. Cardamon comes from Cardamon Inc., per its website.

Does NudoIQ work in Spanish?

Yes. NudoIQ's buttons and banners on the load board work in English and Spanish. Telegram messages are in English. Cardamon's listing language is English (checked October 16, 2026).

How does NudoIQ decide which loads to book?
