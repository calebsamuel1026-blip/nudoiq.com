Amazon Relay vs DAT load board: how they compare

Relay and DAT both put loads in front of carriers, but they work very differently. Here's a side-by-side from each company's own pages.

NudoIQ · Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 14, 2026

Short answer

Amazon Relay is Amazon's own load board: access is free once you're approved, Amazon tenders its own loads directly to carriers, and pay runs on a weekly cycle. DAT is a paid subscription board where brokers and shippers post freight, with carrier plans from $59 a month per dat.com, checked October 14, 2026. Amazon doesn't tender loads through DAT or any other third-party board, so which one fits depends on whether you qualify for Relay and what freight you want.

What's the main difference between Amazon Relay and DAT?

Who posts the freight. On Relay, Amazon tenders its own loads straight to carriers, plus what Amazon calls "Newer load options like hostler and shuttle and third-party shipper loads." On DAT, brokers and shippers post loads for carriers to find.

How much does Amazon Relay cost compared to DAT?

Relay is free to use. Amazon's home page says Relay gives "trucking companies of all sizes free access to instantly book spot loads and contracts."

DAT charges a monthly subscription. Per DAT's pricing section, checked October 14, 2026, carrier plans were:

  • DAT One Standard: $59 a month, with "up to 500 load searches and truck posts per month"
  • DAT One Enhanced: $149 a month
  • DAT One Pro: $169 a month
  • DAT One Select: $259 a month, plus $169 per additional user
  • DAT One Office: $339 a month, plus $169 per additional user

That day, DAT's page also showed a limited-time "Gear Up Event" upgrade running until October 23, 2026. Prices and offers change, so check DAT's site before you buy.

Amazon's brokers vs direct booking post says traditional load boards "Often require paid subscriptions." That's Amazon's general view of load boards and brokers, not a statement about DAT by name.

How do you get access to each one?

Relay requires approval first. The Relay FAQ asks for a DOT number active at least 180 days, a valid MC number, safety scores within Amazon's BASIC limits and insurance minimums. Our Amazon Relay requirements guide has the full list. After approval, access depends on how you perform: Amazon's comparison table lists "Performance-based access to loads" for Relay.

DAT is a subscription. You pick a plan and pay. DAT also sells operating authority services, factoring, a fuel card and insurance, per its product menu. DAT doesn't publish a minimum authority age on the pages we checked.

Who posts the loads, and does it matter?

On Relay, Amazon is the shipper. Amazon says it doesn't tender loads through any third-party load boards, and that Amazon loads "will only be tendered directly to approved and onboarded carriers via the Relay web portal or Relay mobile app."

Amazon's brokers post claims that brokers "take a finder's fee for each load (usually 13-15%)." That's Amazon's claim about brokers in general.

On DAT, you deal with brokers and shippers. Here's where DAT does well. Per DAT's load boards page, it offers "More loads than any other load board, plus company reviews and credit scores," and it says "more than 291 million loads & trucks posted annually." Per DAT's carriers page, carriers get "access to 722,500 loads posted every business day."

Amazon doesn't publish a load count that matches DAT's "posted every business day" number, so the two can't be compared directly. Amazon's peak season post forecasts 300 thousand loads per week by December 2026.

How does pay work on Relay and DAT?

Relay pays on a set schedule. The FAQ says: "Payment cycle runs weekly from Sunday-Saturday. Everything completed before 23:59 Saturday will be paid out the following week on Friday." Amazon also contrasts this with "30-60 day waits for payouts from logistics brokers," which is Amazon's claim about brokers in general. Our payments guide covers invoices and disputes.

On DAT, pay terms depend on each broker. DAT doesn't publish one schedule. Its higher plans include DAT Assurance, where "Our team helps carriers with past-due freight payments."

How do Relay and DAT handle fraud?

Amazon's comparison table lists three controls for Relay: "Verified carriers only," "No double brokering" and "Identity verification." Because Amazon doesn't tender loads through third-party boards, an "Amazon load" offered on another board wasn't tendered by Amazon.

DAT gives you tools to check the other side: company reviews and credit scores on brokers.

Our Amazon Relay fraud guide covers double brokering, fake loads and the red flags Amazon lists.

Which one fits your trucking business?

It depends on your situation, not on which board is "better."

  • If you can't meet Relay's requirements yet, Relay isn't open to you until you do. DAT is a paid subscription instead of an approval.
  • If you qualify for Relay, you get Amazon's freight free, with weekly pay and access tied to your performance.
  • If you want broker freight from many shippers, that's DAT's market.

What doesn't either side publish?

  • A Relay load count that matches DAT's per-business-day number
  • DAT's payment terms, which depend on each broker
  • A rate comparison between the two
  • Whether DAT requires a minimum authority age

Frequently asked questions

Is Amazon Relay free compared to DAT?

Yes. Amazon says Relay gives trucking companies free access once approved. DAT carrier plans started at $59 a month per dat.com, checked October 14, 2026.

Can I find Amazon Relay loads on DAT?

Amazon says it does not tender loads through any third-party load boards. Amazon loads are tendered only to approved carriers on the Relay portal or app.

Is Amazon Relay better than DAT?

Neither is better for every carrier. Relay is free but needs approval and only carries Amazon freight plus some third-party shipper loads, while DAT is paid and carries broker and shipper freight.

Does DAT pay faster than Amazon Relay?

On DAT, your pay comes from each broker on its own terms. Relay pays weekly: work completed by 23:59 Saturday is paid the following Friday, per Amazon's FAQ.

How many loads does DAT have compared to Relay?

DAT says 722,500 loads are posted every business day. Amazon doesn't publish a matching number; it says thousands of loads in regular periods.

What does a DAT load board subscription cost per month?

Per dat.com on October 14, 2026, carrier plans were $59, $149, $169, $259 and $339 a month, with $169 per additional user on Select and Office. Check DAT's site, since prices change.
