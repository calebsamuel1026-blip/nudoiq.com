Can you use a cargo van for Amazon Relay?

Short answer, no. Here's Amazon's exact wording, the smallest truck it does accept, what our FMCSA data shows about the vehicles hauling Amazon freight, and the routes into Relay that actually exist.

NudoIQ · Published October 8, 2026 · Last updated October 8, 2026

Short answer

No. Amazon's truck requirements page says "Cargo vans cannot be used for loads on Amazon Relay." The smallest equipment Amazon supports is a 16 ft dock-height box truck with a working tuck-under liftgate. Sprinter vans, pickup trucks and hotshot rigs aren't on Amazon's supported equipment list either.

What does Amazon say about cargo vans on Relay?

Amazon says it in one line on its truck requirements page: "Cargo vans cannot be used for loads on Amazon Relay." The Relay FAQ lists the same equipment and no vans.

So there's no van category to apply under, and no van loads to book. It doesn't matter how big the van is or how much it can carry. If it's a cargo van, it's out.

What equipment does Amazon Relay accept?

This is Amazon's full supported list:

  • Tractors: day cabs (short-distance) and sleeper cabs (long haul).
  • Box trucks: 26 ft and 16 ft, dock height (also called straight trucks), with a functional tuck-under liftgate.
  • Trailers: 48 ft trailers, 53 ft dry vans and 53 ft reefers. Reefers must meet the FDA Sanitary Transportation Rule.
  • Containers: 20, 40 and 45 ft high cube, UIIA compliant, for drayage from rail and sea ports.

Two more things from the same page. Most Relay loads are power only: you bring the tractor and Relay supplies the trailer. And there's no fleet size requirement, so one truck can be enough.

Can you use a sprinter van, pickup or hotshot for Amazon Relay?

Not on the list. Amazon's page doesn't name sprinter vans, pickup trucks or hotshot rigs at all. It lists what's supported, and none of them are on it.

So if you run a hotshot or a pickup today, that rig isn't on the list Amazon supports. Plan around equipment that is.

If you own a cargo van, what are your options for Relay?

Three paths exist, and each one starts with supported equipment.

Run a box truck

A 16 ft or 26 ft dock-height box truck with a working tuck-under liftgate is the smallest way in. Box trucks can book spot loads, one-way loads and contracts, including 8-hour and 13-hour solo contracts with round trips. Our Amazon Relay box truck guide covers the truck, and how Amazon Relay contracts work covers booking weeks of work ahead.

Rent the equipment

Amazon says "Discounts are available for renting box trucks and tractors for a load." The terms are on Amazon's discounts page, so check them there before you plan around a rental.

Go power only with a tractor

Most Relay loads are power only. A day cab or sleeper tractor hooks to an Amazon trailer, so you don't need to own a trailer for most of the work. Read how Amazon Relay power only works.

What else do you need besides the right truck?

The equipment is only one gate. The carrier account has its own rules, and they're the same whatever truck you run:

  • A DOT number with interstate authority, active for at least 180 days, and a valid MC number.
  • An FMCSA safety rating of Satisfactory, None or Not Rated.
  • Insurance, including $1,000,000 auto liability per occurrence and at least $100,000 cargo.
  • Verification of the business and every beneficial owner (anyone with more than 25%).

The full checklist is in our Amazon Relay requirements guide, with coverages in the insurance requirements guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can you use a sprinter van for Amazon Relay?

No. Sprinter vans aren't on Amazon's supported equipment list, and Amazon says cargo vans can't be used for Relay loads. The smallest supported truck is a 16 ft dock-height box truck with a working tuck-under liftgate.

Can you use a pickup truck for Amazon Relay?

Pickup trucks aren't on Amazon's supported equipment list. What Relay lists is day cab and sleeper tractors, 16 ft and 26 ft dock-height box trucks with a tuck-under liftgate, trailers and high cube containers.

Can you do Amazon Relay with a hotshot?

Hotshot rigs aren't on Amazon's supported equipment list, so plan on a tractor or a box truck instead. Most Relay loads are power only, which means a day cab or sleeper tractor pulling an Amazon trailer.

Is there an Amazon Relay load board for cargo vans?

No. Because Amazon says cargo vans can't be used on Relay, there are no van loads on the board. The smallest equipment that gets you onto the board is a 16 ft dock-height box truck with a working tuck-under liftgate.

What is the smallest truck you can use for Amazon Relay?

A 16 ft box truck. It has to be dock height with a functional tuck-under liftgate. A 26 ft box truck with the same features also qualifies.

Does Amazon help with renting a truck for Relay?

Amazon says discounts are available for renting box trucks and tractors for a load. The terms are on its discounts page at relay.amazon.com/discounts.
