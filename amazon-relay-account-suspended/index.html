Amazon Relay account suspended or can't book loads? What to check

Getting locked out of Relay stops your income, and Amazon says little about why. Here's what Amazon publishes, what to check first, who to contact, and what nobody outside Amazon can answer.

NudoIQ · Published September 20, 2026 · Last updated October 2, 2026

Short answer

Amazon doesn't publish suspension rules, appeal steps or reinstatement timelines. What it does publish: an insurance lapse blocks booking and hauling until coverage is reinstated, and Amazon monitors carrier compliance through internal teams and a third-party service. Check your Insurance tab and authority first, then contact the Support Center from your Relay account.

Why can't I book loads on Amazon Relay?

The most common reason Amazon itself names is insurance. Amazon says that if your coverage lapses, your company can't book or haul Relay loads until it's reinstated.

Beyond that, Amazon's FAQ says it uses internal compliance teams and a third-party compliance monitoring service to validate and continually monitor carriers. It doesn't list which problems lead to which action. So start with the things you can check yourself:

  • Insurance: every policy current in the Insurance tab of your Relay account.
  • Authority: your DOT and MC numbers active on FMCSA's SAFER site.
  • Messages: any notice or email from Amazon, and the exact wording you see when you try to book.

If the board just looks empty and you can still book, that's a different problem. See Relay load board not showing loads.

Is my Relay account suspended, or is it something else?

Not every lockout is a suspension. Several published Relay rules limit what you can book without shutting the account:

The grade rules come from Amazon's performance grades guide. Every grade keeps load board access, so a low grade alone doesn't take the board away. Our scorecard guide explains the four metrics.

For drivers, Amazon says profiles are created only by invitation from an approved carrier, and it reviews the CDL and a selfie, typically within 48 hours.

What does Amazon check to keep a carrier active?

Amazon publishes its carrier requirements and keeps monitoring compliance after approval. It doesn't say which slip triggers what, so check each requirement.

From the Relay FAQ:

  • A DOT number with interstate authority, active at least 180 days, and a valid MC number.
  • Entity type Carrier, authorized for Property and For-Hire.
  • FMCSA safety rating of Satisfactory, None or Not Rated.
  • BASIC scores under Amazon's limits: Unsafe Driving and HOS Compliance below 60%; Vehicle Maintenance, Controlled Substances/Alcohol and Driver Fitness below 75%.
  • Driver out-of-service rate at or below 35% and vehicle violation rate at or below 50%.
  • Verification of the business and every owner with more than 25%.
  • Insurance at or above the minimums.

The full list is on our Amazon Relay requirements checklist.

How do I fix an insurance lapse on Relay?

Renew the policy, then get the new details into your Relay account. Amazon tells carriers to renew two to three weeks before expiry so RMIS can update records, and to add updated details in the Insurance tab at least 5 days before the policy expires.

If it already lapsed:

  1. Renew or replace the policy with your agent.
  2. Make sure your new certificate reaches RMIS.
  3. Update the Insurance tab with the new dates.
  4. Contact the Support Center and say what you updated.

Amazon's coverage minimums, policy by policy, are on our insurance requirements page.

Who do I contact when my Relay account is suspended?

Use the Support Center in your Relay account. Amazon's FAQ sends account-access problems there when you're logged in. For problems with a load in progress, the Relay Operations Center (ROC) offers 24/7 support through the Relay portal or Relay mobile.

Amazon doesn't publish a support phone number, or a separate route if you can't log in at all. Be careful with any number or "reinstatement service" you find elsewhere that isn't on relay.amazon.com.

Can I appeal an Amazon Relay suspension?

Amazon doesn't publish an appeal process. There are no published steps, deadlines, limits on how many times you can ask, or success rates. The Support Center is the only channel Amazon names for account problems.

Before you write in, have these ready so you can explain it once:

  • The exact message or email from Amazon, with the date.
  • A screenshot of what you see when you try to book.
  • Your current certificate of insurance.
  • Your DOT and MC numbers and their status on SAFER.

How long does an Amazon Relay suspension last?

Amazon doesn't say. It publishes no reinstatement timeline. For insurance, booking stays blocked until coverage is reinstated, so update the Insurance tab fast.

Can I reapply to Amazon Relay after being rejected or deactivated?

For rejected applications, yes in some cases. The FAQ says you may be eligible to reapply once a fixable issue like insurance coverage, authority status or missing documentation is resolved, and that serious compliance issues may disqualify you.

Amazon doesn't publish a waiting period, and it says nothing about reapplying after an active account is deactivated. Ask the Support Center for your case.

Can a low scorecard get my Relay account suspended?

Amazon doesn't list the scorecard as a suspension reason. It does tie your grade to what you can book. Your overall score equals your lowest of four metrics (on-time, acceptance, app usage and disruption-free) over the last six weeks. A drop can cost you contracts, auctions and early access to spot loads. You can dispute score impacts from your Relay account, and Amazon decides each one.

Can a load board extension or dispatcher get my account suspended?

Nobody outside Amazon can honestly answer that. Amazon doesn't publish what triggers a suspension, so no one can promise any tool, dispatcher or setting will or won't affect your account. Your account is your responsibility: read the Relay terms you agreed to, and never hand your Amazon password to anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a phone number for Amazon Relay support?

Amazon doesn't publish one. Account problems go to the Support Center in your Relay account, and load problems go to the Relay Operations Center through the portal or Relay mobile.

How long does it take to get reinstated on Amazon Relay?

Amazon doesn't publish a reinstatement timeline. For an insurance lapse, booking stays blocked until coverage is reinstated, so update the Insurance tab as soon as you renew.

Why does Relay say I can't book loads?

Amazon's published reason is an insurance lapse. It also monitors compliance through internal teams and a third-party service. Check your insurance and authority, then contact the Support Center.

Can I appeal a Relay deactivation?

Amazon doesn't publish appeal steps, deadlines or success rates. Contact the Support Center with your documents and the exact message you received.

Can I reapply after my Relay application was rejected?

Possibly. Amazon says you may reapply once a fixable issue like insurance, authority or missing documents is resolved. Serious compliance issues may disqualify you. No waiting period is published.

Does a low grade block me from the Relay load board?

No. Amazon says every grade keeps load board access. Lower grades lose new contracts (below B), auctions (below C+) and early access to spot loads.
