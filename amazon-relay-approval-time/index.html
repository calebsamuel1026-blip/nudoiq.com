How long does it take to get approved for Amazon Relay?

You've sent the application and now you're waiting. Here's what Amazon says about the timeline, what usually holds it up, and what to do if it stalls or comes back rejected.

NudoIQ · Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 14, 2026

Short answer

Amazon's Relay FAQ says a completed application is typically approved or rejected within 1 to 3 weeks, and that any delay is due to validating insurance information. Amazon's blog posts give slightly different ranges: within three weeks, and 2 to 3 weeks. If you hear nothing after two weeks, check that your FMCSA contact information matches your Relay application exactly.

How long does Amazon Relay approval take?

Usually one to three weeks. Amazon's Relay FAQ says that once Amazon receives your completed application, "it will typically be approved or rejected within 1-3 weeks."

Amazon's own pages don't all give the same range, though:

Amazon pageWhat it says
Relay FAQApproved or rejected within 1 to 3 weeks
How to become a carrier"Amazon generally reviews completed applications within three weeks."
Prime Day postApproval "can take 2-3 weeks"
High volume events post"about 1-3 weeks"

So plan on up to three weeks. And note the word "completed." The clock starts when Amazon has everything, not when you open the form.

Filling out the form is the quick part. Amazon's carrier post says you can submit everything "in as little as 20 minutes." You'll give your DOT number and company details, verify a one-time password, list your equipment and add payment information. What you need to qualify is in our Amazon Relay requirements guide.

In Canada it's faster on paper. The relay.amazon.ca page says applications are approved or rejected within 2 to 4 business days.

What slows a Relay application down?

Insurance, by Amazon's own account. The FAQ says: "Delays, if any, will be due to validation of insurance information."

What Amazon tells you to do about it:

  • Have it all ready first. Amazon "urges you to have all data ready prior to applying."
  • Call your insurance provider if it stalls. The FAQ says to reach out to them in the event of any delays.
  • Get your insurance on file with FMCSA. If your insurance gets flagged, Amazon says to "ensure your provider submits the information directly to FMCSA."
  • Give new insurance time. If you're already a Relay carrier and changing insurance, the high volume events post recommends allowing 2 weeks for it to process.

Our insurance requirements guide lists the minimums your certificate needs to show.

Can you apply before your DOT number is 180 days old?

Yes. The FAQ says Relay "typically requires your DOT number to be at least 180 days old to complete onboarding," but "we encourage you to apply early."

Amazon's reason: starting now lets it begin reviewing your information, so you're ready to move forward as soon as you meet the requirement.

Haven't heard back after two weeks?

Check your FMCSA record first. The FAQ says to check "that your FMCSA contact information matches your Relay application details, down to the dash or comma."

That detail matters because "Our onboarding team uses your information on file with the FMCSA to reach you." If the phone number or email on file is old, Amazon's messages may never reach you.

If everything matches and you still haven't heard, click Contact Us on the top right of your application page. The FAQ also says Amazon Relay doesn't have an official contact phone number, so don't go looking for one.

You can watch progress from your phone. Amazon's carrier post says to "download the Relay app on iOS or Android to get started and track your application's progress on the go."

Can you reapply if Amazon Relay rejects your application?

Often, yes. The FAQ says: "In many cases, you may be eligible to reapply." Amazon recommends first reviewing the reason for your rejection.

  • Fixable reasons. For issues "like insurance coverage, authority status, or missing documentation, you're welcome to reapply once it's been addressed."
  • Serious reasons. Amazon says some rejection reasons, such as serious compliance issues, may disqualify you from applying again.

Amazon doesn't publish a waiting period before you reapply. Fix the cause first. If it was insurance, have your provider submit it to FMCSA before you send a new application.

What happens after you're approved?

The FAQ says you'll get an email with instructions to access your Relay account. Then:

  1. Complete the Relay 101 course in Relay's Learning Center (about 10 minutes).
  2. Add your drivers and equipment to your Relay profile. Each driver is invited and verified separately.
  3. Start booking through the Relay load board.

Before the first booking, work out what a load leaves you. Our free Relay load calculator takes a load's pay and miles and shows your net and net per loaded mile, deadhead included.

What doesn't Amazon publish about approval?

  • What each application status, such as "in review," means
  • Any way to speed up a review
  • A waiting period before you reapply
  • A full list of rejection reasons
  • An approval rate

If someone promises a faster approval, ask where that's written on relay.amazon.com.

Frequently asked questions

How long does Amazon Relay take to approve an application?

Amazon's Relay FAQ says a completed application is typically approved or rejected within 1 to 3 weeks. Other Amazon posts say within three weeks or 2 to 3 weeks, so plan on up to three. Delays come from insurance validation.

How do I check my Amazon Relay application status?

Amazon says you can track your application's progress in the Relay app on iOS or Android. It doesn't publish what each status means. If you hear nothing in two weeks, check your FMCSA contact details, then click Contact Us on your application page.

Why was my Amazon Relay application rejected?

Amazon tells you to start with the rejection reason it gives you. Its FAQ names insurance coverage, authority status and missing documentation as fixable reasons, and says serious compliance issues may disqualify you from applying again. It doesn't publish a full list.

Can I reapply to Amazon Relay after a rejection?

In many cases, you can. Amazon's FAQ says you're welcome to reapply once a fixable issue like insurance coverage, authority status or missing documentation is addressed. It publishes no waiting period.

Does Amazon Relay accept new authority?

Relay typically requires your DOT number to be at least 180 days old to complete onboarding. Amazon's FAQ still encourages you to apply early, so it can start reviewing your information before you meet the requirement.

How long does Amazon Relay approval take in Canada?

The relay.amazon.ca page says Canadian applications are approved or rejected within 2 to 4 business days, with any delay due to insurance validation.
