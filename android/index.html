Use NudoIQ on your phone

Chrome extensions do not run natively on Android. Lemur Browser changes that. It downloads from Google Play and runs Chrome Web Store apps exactly as they work on your computer.

This route has not been tested on every Android phone. NudoIQ is built for a computer and some features may not work exactly as they do on desktop. If you see no changes after adding the extension, you likely need a computer to use NudoIQ fully.

1. Install Lemur Browser

Open Google Play Store on your phone. Search for Lemur Browser. Tap Install. The app is free and replaces older options like Kiwi.

2. Find the Chrome Web Store

Open Lemur. Tap the menu. Go to chromewebstore.google.com. You may need to sign in with your Google account.

3. Add NudoIQ

Search NudoIQ. Tap the NudoIQ card. Press Add to Chrome. Confirm the popup. The process is identical to adding an extension on your laptop.

4. Sign in to Amazon Relay

In Lemur, go to relay.amazon.com. Sign in with your Amazon credentials. Reload the page if the board does not appear right away.

5. The NudoIQ bar is live

The NudoIQ controls appear above the load board. Auto-refresh, Instant Book, AutoBook, lane prices, and scorecard dispute letters are ready to use.

Near a computer instead?

On a computer, no extra app is needed. Install NudoIQ directly from the Chrome Web Store and open it on relay.amazon.com. You get the full experience with no setup.
