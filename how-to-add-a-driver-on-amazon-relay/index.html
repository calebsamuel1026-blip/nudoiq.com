How do you add a driver on Amazon Relay?

For carrier owners: how a driver gets onto your Relay account, what the driver does next, how loads get assigned, and how to keep people you didn't approve out.

NudoIQ · Published October 23, 2026 · Last updated October 23, 2026

Short answer

You invite the driver. Amazon says drivers can only create Relay profiles through invitations from Relay-approved carriers. The driver signs in to the Relay app with an Amazon account, enters CDL and contact details, and must pass the identity check.

How do drivers get on your Relay account?

By invite only. After approval, Amazon's Relay FAQ says: "Add your drivers and equipment to your Relay profile." It also says: "A carrier needs to invite their drivers to create a profile in the Relay app."

Amazon's driver eligibility post is just as clear: "Drivers can only create Relay profiles through invitations from Relay-approved carriers."

Amazon's public pages don't show the click-by-click steps for sending an invite. What they do show is what happens after it.

What does the driver do after the invite?

  1. Open the Relay app. Log in to an existing Amazon account or create a new one.
  2. Enter the details. CDL and contact information.
  3. Take the photos. The license front and back, plus a selfie.
  4. Pass the checks. "Only drivers who successfully complete an identification check can haul Relay loads."

Amazon typically reviews the information within 48 hours. Each driver needs their own login: "Drivers can't share their accounts." What each check looks at is in our background check guide, and the driver's side is in Amazon Relay for drivers.

What are you responsible for as the carrier?

More than the invite. Amazon says "Carriers are responsible for their own drivers," which covers screening, hours of service, medical certification and license checks.

Two more lines from Amazon to keep in mind:

  • "Carriers should only assign Amazon loads to drivers who have successfully passed background checks."
  • "Multiple failed selfie checks can impact a driver's and carrier's access to Relay."

So don't promise a driver a load until they've cleared.

How do you assign a Relay load to a driver?

You or your dispatcher book the load on the carrier's account, then put it on an invited driver who passed the checks. The driver runs it in the Relay app.

Keep the app open with location on for the whole trip. App usage is one of the four metrics in your performance score, so one driver who closes the app can pull your grade down. Our app guide covers the driver side.

Can you add a dispatcher to Amazon Relay?

Amazon's own security advice suggests you can. Its email scams post tells carriers to check their "Admin/dispatcher list: Is everyone someone you approved?"

But Amazon doesn't publish the steps or the role names for adding a dispatcher user. The rules that do apply:

  • A dispatcher works inside the carrier's own account.
  • Never share or resell access to your account.
  • No subcontracting or double brokering.

More on the job itself in our dispatcher guide.

How do you keep strangers off your Relay account?

Log in and check it often. The same Amazon post lists what to look at:

CheckWhat to ask
Driver list"Do you recognize every driver?"
Admin/dispatcher listIs everyone someone you approved?
Recent loadsDid your company book each one?
Bank detailsAre they still yours?

Amazon warns that scammers who get in "can access your account, add unauthorized drivers, and book loads they intend to steal." And: "Amazon Relay will never request Relay carrier's sensitive account information, password or the one-time password." See our fraud guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can a driver sign up for Amazon Relay on their own?

No. Amazon says drivers can only create Relay profiles through invitations from Relay-approved carriers.

How long does Amazon Relay driver verification take?

Amazon says it typically reviews the CDL and selfie within 48 hours. The driver can't haul Relay loads until the identity check is passed.

Can two drivers share one Relay login?

No. Amazon says drivers can't share their accounts, so each driver needs their own profile.

How do I add a dispatcher to my Amazon Relay account?

Amazon doesn't publish the steps or role names. Its security post tells carriers to review their admin/dispatcher list and make sure everyone on it is someone they approved.

Will Amazon Relay ask for my password?

No. Amazon says Relay will never request a carrier's sensitive account information, password or one-time password.
