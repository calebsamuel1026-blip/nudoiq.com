Refund Policy
NudoIQ Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: June 28, 2026

Free Trial

Every new subscription includes a 7-day free trial. Your card is required to start but you are not charged until the trial period ends. Cancel any time before day 7 and you will never be billed.

All Sales Are Final

Once the free trial ends and billing begins, all charges are final. We do not issue refunds for any paid billing period, whether or not the extension was actively used during that period.

This policy exists because the subscription provides immediate, ongoing access to load intelligence data and AI features that are consumed the moment access is granted.

No Partial-Period Refunds

Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. We do not issue prorated or partial refunds for unused days remaining in a billing period after cancellation.

How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through Stripe, the payment processor we use. Cancellation stops all future charges immediately — your access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.

To cancel, visit your Stripe billing portal or email contact@nudoiq.com and we will cancel on your behalf within one business day.

Billing Disputes

If you believe you were charged in error — for example, if you were billed after cancelling, or charged more than once — contact us before initiating a chargeback. We resolve legitimate billing errors promptly.

Email contact@nudoiq.com with your email address and a description of the issue. We will respond within 2 business days.

Contact

Questions about your subscription or this policy? Reach us at contact@nudoiq.com or on Telegram.
